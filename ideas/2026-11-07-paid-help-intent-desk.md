# Paid Help Intent Desk for AI Service Solos

**Date:** November 7, 2026

## X signal (today)

X on 6-7 November 2026 shows freelancers and solo builders hunting the same signal: people who post "can someone help me set this up, I'll pay" and then get buried.

- JoePro (@JoePro, 6 Nov): Built a bot that searches public posts for people asking for paid help with AI agents, coding and automation. It returns up to 5 fresh leads with source, stated budget (or "unknown"), drops already-seen threads, and writes a reply draft. The human still decides whether to send. He offered the bot publicly.
- Adjacent threads: people recommend simple X searches like "is there a way to automate" or "need someone to automate" and reply with a quick fix then an offer. Multiple posts about turning open-source social readers (Agent Reach and similar) into paid daily intent lists.
- Broader pattern this week: productized AI automation services (lead qualification, research briefs, support triage) are repeatedly named as the practical path for solo operators. The bottleneck is finding the buyers who already said they will pay, not building another generic agent.

## Concept

Sell a **daily or weekly paid-help intent pack** to freelancers, solo AI-agent builders, and small automation agencies who want warm leads without spending hours on X search or getting spam replies.

The customer defines:

- their offer (e.g. "set up Claude Code + custom agents for Shopify stores", "n8n workflows for lead qualification", "Grok/Claude agent for inbox triage")
- platforms and keywords (X + Reddit by default)
- exclusion rules (already contacted, certain niches, minimum engagement)

Agents plus one human operator return each morning:

1. Up to 5 fresh public posts where someone asked for paid help that matches the offer.
2. Source URL, timestamp, author, exact quote of the ask, and any stated budget or timeline.
3. A short context note (thread replies, whether it looks real vs spam, engagement).
4. One draft reply in the customer's voice (or a neutral professional voice) that offers a specific next step. Never auto-sent.
5. A "already seen" filter so the same thread never reappears.
6. Source appendix. No invented budgets. No private DMs scraped.

This is **not** a full CRM, **not** an auto-outreach bot that posts without review, and **not** a generic social listening dashboard. It is a productized desk that turns public "I'll pay" posts into a short, cited list a human can act on in 15 minutes.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: Solo AI freelancers, one-person automation shops, and indie hackers who already sell agent setup or workflow builds and currently hunt leads by hand on X/Reddit.
- Urgent pain: High-intent posts get buried under spam or ignored; manually searching and drafting takes more time than the work itself.
- Existing workaround: Saved searches, manual browsing, or generic cold outreach that converts poorly.

## Why this works

- Market signal: This week's X posts explicitly describe building or wanting bots that surface "I'll pay for help with AI agents" requests. Open-source tools like Agent Reach already give agents free eyes on X and Reddit.
- Agent advantage: Continuous monitoring, deduplication, quote extraction, budget detection, and first-draft replies. Human judgment stays on the final send.
- Solo-operator advantage: One person who has closed a few paid agent setups can write the reply templates and exclusion rules. The agents do the watching.

## Monetization

- Primary model: per-pack service + optional retainer.
- First price test: 99 EUR / 99 USD for a 7-day pack (daily email or Notion/Slack dump of up to 5 leads). 249 for a 30-day pack.
- Upsell: 149/month retainer (ongoing daily feed + weekly template refresh); 49 rush same-day setup; 29 add-on for one extra platform or niche.
- Fun / public variant: Publish one synthetic pack from public posts (anonymized) as a sample. Never publish real customer filters or contacted handles.

## Validation plan

1. Riskiest assumption: Solo AI freelancers will pay ~99 for a cited daily list of high-intent public posts instead of continuing to search by hand.
2. Demand test: Post one anonymized sample pack on X and in 2-3 indie-hacker / freelance AI rooms on 7-9 Nov. DM 20 people who posted about hunting leads, building outreach bots, or "I'll pay for agent setup." Offer the first 5 packs at 69 / 48h SLA.
3. Success bar: 5 serious replies and 2 paid packs in 14 days. Kill if 20 outbound touches produce zero deposits.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Monitor.** Use Agent Reach (or equivalent free social readers) + targeted searches on X and Reddit. Pull recent posts matching keyword packs.

**Agent 2 — Filter & dedupe.** Drop retweets, already-seen IDs, obvious spam, and posts that do not contain a clear paid-help ask. Extract quote + any budget language.

**Agent 3 — Context & draft.** For each survivor, read the immediate thread, score realism, and write one short reply draft matching the customer's voice notes.

**Agent 4 — Pack assembler.** Format the 5-item list with sources, quotes, drafts, and a "seen" log. Human reviews before any send.

**Human operator.** Approve or edit drafts, maintain the exclusion list, send the final messages, and collect feedback. Agents never post or DM on their own.

Stack to start: Agent Reach or similar open-source readers, one LLM for drafting, a simple spreadsheet or Notion for the seen log, Stripe Payment Link, email or Slack delivery. No paid social APIs required in week one.

## Risks and mitigations

- Auto-sending spam: Agents only draft. Human must click send. Document this clearly.
- Scraping private data: Only public posts. No login-walled content. Cite the public URL.
- Overlap with generic outreach tools: This pack is specifically for explicit "I'll pay for help" intent, not cold lists. Sell the citation and the draft quality.
- Platform changes: Agent Reach and similar tools break occasionally. Have a manual fallback search for the first two weeks.
- Low volume: If a niche has fewer than 3 qualifying posts per day, expand keywords or switch to weekly packs. Do not invent leads.
