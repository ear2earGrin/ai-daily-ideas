## Concept

Sell a **same-week persona landing eval pack** when a solopreneur, freelancer, or 1-5 person shop has a live landing page (or agent marketplace listing) and currently decides copy by founder taste or one ChatGPT roast.

The customer uploads or points at:

- the live URL plus optional variant URLs or screenshots
- who they *think* the buyer is (notes, ads, last 10 sales, or "I have no idea")
- last 7 days of traffic / form / checkout notes they are willing to share (Plausible, Stripe, Typeform export, or a Loom)
- optional: one competitor page they keep losing to

Agents plus one human operator return within 48 hours:

1. Three persona cards grounded in their notes or marked `draft-unverified`: job, constraint, what they fear buying, what would make them click.
2. A page scorecard per persona: promise, proof, price clarity, next action, trust objects. Cite the section or screenshot. No invented conversion rates.
3. A break-list: where the page (or the product behind it) fails that persona the way a generic agent fails a dentist front desk — missing hours, missing price, missing "who reviews this," missing refund path.
4. A 7-line change card: three copy/CTA edits the owner may ship this week, two that stay human-only (price, guarantee, legal), one test they should *not* run.
5. A first-week script: ship one variant for persona A only; do not generate a cinematic ad until the form actually converts once.
6. A source appendix. No fake ROAS. No "this will 3x your close rate."

This is **not** a hosted A/B platform, **not** an ad-buying agent, and **not** a video studio. It is a productized desk for *one page, three personas, one week*, so taste has a file.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US indie hackers and small shops launching an agent, a micro-SaaS, or a service page this month who have traffic or a waitlist but no named buyer on the page.
- Urgent pain: they keep rewriting the hero in Cursor, posting another demo, and cannot say which sentence a freight broker would distrust.

## Why this works

Existing catalog packs cover shipping the artifact and making the shop visible to *buyer agents* — not scoring the human landing page against named humans.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept URL + notes + optional exports. Detect one page. Refuse to eval a 40-page marketing site in one pack.

**Agent 2 — Persona clerk.** Build three personas only from their notes, last sales, or marked `draft-unverified`. Never invent a "busy millennial mom" with no source.

**Agent 3 — Page walker.** Score promise / proof / price / CTA / trust per persona. Quote the line. Flag missing hours, missing human review, missing price as break-list items.

**Agent 4 — Pack writer.** Persona cards, scorecard, break-list, 7-line change card, first-week script. Every metric cites an export or `draft-unverified`.

**Human operator.** Kill fake lift math, drop tracking IDs, export PDF + Markdown. The model does not push to Webflow and does not spend ad budget.

Stack to start: coding + browser agents, one LLM, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. Public URL + notes only in week one.

## Risks and mitigations

- Acting as their media buyer or designer: never connect to ads or CMS. Draft the change card. They paste.
- Overlap with ship-receipt distribution (2026-09-23): that pack is what to post after a ship. This pack is whether the destination page matches a named buyer.
- Overlap with buyer-agent visibility (2026-09-24): that pack is llms.txt / MCP so *software* buyers can find them. This pack is humans on the landing page.
- Overlap with one-job charter (2026-09-27): that pack writes the agent's job. This pack writes who the *page* is hiring the visitor to become.
- Overlap with problem-intent reply (2026-09-09): that pack answers inbound posts. This pack scores the owned page.
- Thin notes: if they send no buyer evidence, all three personas must be labeled `draft-unverified` on line one.
- Scope creep into shooting the persona movie: deliver the eval. Stop.
