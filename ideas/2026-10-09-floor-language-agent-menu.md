## Concept

Sell a **same-day floor-language agent menu** to a local owner (clinic, salon, trade, small shop) who is not on X and is about to either ignore agents or buy a 10-minute listing they cannot describe.

The customer sends:

- a WhatsApp export or a voice-note dump from the last 7 days of customer and supplier chats. Photos optional. No full phone backup.
- one sentence on what they do and how many people are on the floor today.
- optional: one agent listing, ad, or DM they were sent (the 10–79 USD "set up in 10 minutes" page is the default bait).
- optional: the one job they wish would stop landing in their personal chat after 6pm.

Agents plus one human operator return within one business day:

1. A shop-language restatement of the last 7 days: the repeated asks, in the owner's words, with counts. No invented volume.
2. Three jobs an agent could take this month, each with the human gate (who approves a send, a price, a booking). One job only if the dump does not support three.
3. A skip line on the listing they pasted: what it claims, what the page actually installs, and whether it is an IF/THEN wrapper. `not-on-page` if the listing does not say.
4. A WhatsApp message they can forward to a vendor or a freelancer, naming the one job, the gate, and the do-not-touch list (refunds, discounts, medical advice, anything over a named amount).
5. A source appendix. No claim that the menu installed an agent, saw their books, or guarantees hours saved.

This is **not** an agent install, **not** a chatbot on their WhatsApp, and **not** a quote for a retainer they have not asked for. It is a productized desk that makes the first buy inspectable in the language of the floor.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: the owner-operator of a local shop who still runs the day from WhatsApp and has been pitched an agent they cannot explain to the person on the till.
- Urgent pain: X is loud about agent teams. Their room is not. A 10-minute listing does not name the job that actually piles up after 6pm.
- Existing workaround: ignore the pitch, forward it to a nephew, or pay for a setup that replies to the wrong chat.

## Why this works

- Market signal: the 9 Oct room-versus-timeline post is the buyer gap. The 9 Oct WhatsApp-native ask is the channel. The 7 Oct 10–79 USD listing and the IF/THEN critique are the thing they are about to buy or skip.
- Agent advantage: read a chat export and a listing page, count repeated asks, refuse any hour-saved number the dump does not support, write the forwardable WhatsApp in their register.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has stood in a shop can tell a job that belongs on the floor from a wrapper. The model is the clerk. Judgment is which one job is allowed on the menu.

## Monetization

- Primary model: per-menu service, plus a weekly retainer if they want the one chosen job watched after install.
- First price test: 79 EUR for one 7-day dump and one listing, same-day. 129 if the operator also drafts the vendor WhatsApp and a 7-day check-in. 49 for a shop with three or fewer people on the floor, capped at one job.
- Upsell: 39 EUR to rescore a second listing; 149/month to watch the one job they bought (exceptions only, no new chatbot). The menu does not install, log into WhatsApp, or take payments.
- Fun / public variant: publish one anonymized menu from a consented salon or trade dump, with the skip line on a real public 10-minute listing. That sample is the ad.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept a WhatsApp export, a voice-note transcript, and one listing URL. Refuse full phone backups, card numbers, and medical records. Strip phone numbers from the pack before it leaves the desk.

**Agent 2 — Dump clerk.** Count repeated customer asks in the 7-day window. Quote the owner's words. If a count is not in the export, write `not-in-record`.

**Agent 3 — Listing clerk.** Read only the pasted listing. Quote the setup claim, the price, and the job it names. If it does not name a job, say so.

**Agent 4 — Menu writer.** Three jobs max, each with a human gate and a do-not-touch line. One WhatsApp the owner can forward.

**Agent 5 — Pack writer.** Restatement, menu, skip line, forwardable message, appendix.

**Human operator.** Delete any hours-saved claim. Export Markdown. The model does not message the owner's customers or install anything.

Stack to start: one LLM, a paste inbox, a public listing read, a Stripe Payment Link. No WhatsApp Business token in week one.

## Risks and mitigations

- Fake savings: if the dump does not show hours, do not invent them. Write `not-in-record`.
- Private chats: process in isolation, strip numbers, delete after 14 days unless they opt in. Do not train on the dump.
- Overlap with quote-gap card (2026-10-07): that pack scores a seller's automation quote. This pack is the owner's first buy-or-skip menu. Do not merge the files.
- Overlap with missed-call booking (2026-09-07) and weekend inquiry dumps (2026-09-08): those packs finish a front-desk pile. This pack decides whether an agent should touch that pile at all.
- Overlap with counterparty card (2026-10-02): that pack is due diligence on a hire. This pack is the job list before a hire exists.
- Account safety: do not take the owner's WhatsApp login in week one.
- ToS: read an export they send and a public listing only.
