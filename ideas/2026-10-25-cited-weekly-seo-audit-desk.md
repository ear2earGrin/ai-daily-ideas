## Concept

Sell a **cited weekly SEO audit desk** when a local trade (plumber, electrician, HVAC, locksmith, roofer, cleaner) has a website and Google Business Profile but no one who explains, in plain language with citations, what changed last week and which three fixes are worth doing this week.

The customer points at:

- their website URL
- Google Business Profile link
- optional: last 10 reviews or a screenshot of Search Console / GBP insights

Agents plus one human operator return every Monday (or on demand):

1. A one-page scorecard: ranking movement for the 5 most valuable local queries, review velocity, NAP consistency, and mobile speed — every number cites a public source or is marked `draft-unverified`.
2. A three-fix list: the highest-leverage changes (GBP post, service-page title, review reply template, etc.) with exact copy and a “do this first” order.
3. A do-not-touch list: anything the record does not support (invented keywords, competitor claims, etc.).
4. A source appendix. No fabricated rankings. No fake traffic numbers.

This is **not** a full SEO agency, **not** a ranking tool, and **not** a chatbot. It is a productized Monday morning report that a busy owner can act on in 15 minutes.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US solo or 1-5 person local trades who already have a website and GBP but lose hours guessing why calls dried up after a Google update.
- Urgent pain: they pay for ads or a cheap SEO package that never shows the actual weekly delta; they want one clear report instead of a 40-page PDF.
- Existing workaround: ignore the site, ask ChatGPT once a month, or hire a generalist who disappears.

## Why this works

- Market signal: today’s X feed explicitly prices a weekly SEO audit agent at $300/mo with sub-$10 delivery cost; agent marketplaces are trying to make specialized services discoverable.

- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has run local ads or fixed a GBP can write the do-not-touch list and approve the three fixes. Judgment about what a plumber will actually do on a Tuesday is the product; the model is the researcher.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept URL + GBP link + optional screenshots. Detect one location. Refuse multi-brand enterprises.

**Agent 2 — Public scanner.** Fetch visible rankings (or public proxies), review snippets, and on-page signals. Tag every metric `visible-in-record` / `proxy-only` / `not-in-record`.

**Agent 3 — Fix writer.** Produce the three-fix list and do-not-touch list. Every recommendation cites a source or is marked `draft-unverified`.

**Agent 4 — Scorecard assembler.** One-page PDF + Markdown. No invented traffic or conversion numbers.

**Human operator.** Kill fake rankings, drop real customer names from the public sample, export and email the pack. The model does not contact the client.

Stack to start: coding + browser agents, one LLM, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. Public data and screenshots only in week one.

## Risks and mitigations

- Invented rankings: if a number cannot be cited from a public source or client screenshot, the scorecard must say `not-in-record`.
- Overlap with generic SEO agencies: this pack is weekly, cited, and three-fixes-only. Do not offer full content calendars or link building.
- Scope creep into building a ranking tool: deliver the desk. Stop.
- Acting as their SEO manager: never log into their accounts or post as them. Draft only.
