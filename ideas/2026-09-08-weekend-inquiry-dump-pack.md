## Concept

Sell a **same-morning weekend inquiry dump pack** when the shop reopens after a long weekend and the owner is staring at 20-80 unread things.

The customer (clinic, salon, HVAC, plumber, gym, restaurant catering, tutoring, auto shop) uploads:

- Facebook / Instagram / Google / website form exports or screenshots
- WhatsApp / SMS / iMessage threads from the closed days
- missed-call and voicemail list (CSV or phone screenshot)
- current calendar or booking grid for the next 7 days
- optional: price list, service menu, "we do / we don't" note

Agents plus one human operator return before 11:00 local on the reopen day:

1. A ranked lead table: name, channel, ask, urgency, quoted-or-not, suggested first reply.
2. A duplicate map: same person across form + DM + missed call.
3. A do-not-touch list: spam, existing clients mid-job, medical/legal-sensitive, already booked.
4. Five ready-to-send first replies (owner pastes; agents do not post).
5. A capacity warning: how many of the hot leads fit the open slots this week.
6. A source appendix. Every row cites a message, form id, or `not-in-record`.

This is **not** a chatbot on their page, **not** auto-DM, and **not** a CRM migration. It is a clerk that turns the holiday pile into a call order.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US local shops that close public holidays and reopen to a mixed inbox.
- Urgent pain: the owner answers the friendliest DM first and the $900 emergency from Saturday morning goes cold.
- Existing workaround: scrolling Instagram at 07:30, calling whoever they remember, or ignoring the pile until Wednesday.

## Why this works

- Market signal: Labor Day is still on the US trend board while builder Twitter is celebrating agents that process piles overnight. The leftover human job is ranking the pile, not generating more leads.
- Agent advantage: stitch the same person across three channels; refuse to invent a price; keep medical and legal threads out of the paste list.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has worked a front desk can tell a tire-kicker from a same-day job. Judgment is the product.

## Monetization

- First price test: 49 EUR / 49 USD for up to 25 inquiries; 89 for up to 60; 129 above that. Same-morning SLA if intake lands by 08:00 local.
- Upsell: 19 standing voice card (how the shop names services and what they refuse); 199/month retainer covering public-holiday dumps plus one mid-week overflow pack.
- Fun / public variant: publish one synthetic Monday dump (ranked table + five replies, anonymized) after each long weekend as X content.

## Validation plan

1. Riskiest assumption: a shop owner will pay ~50 the morning after a holiday instead of scrolling the pile themselves.
2. Demand test: post one synthetic Labor Day dump pack on X and in 3 local-business groups. DM 25 owners who posted "closed for Labor Day," "back Tuesday," or "sorry for the delay." Offer the first 5 packs at 29 same morning.
3. Success bar: 5 serious replies and 2 paid packs in 14 days (or before the next public holiday). Kill if 25 outbound touches produce zero deposits.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept ZIP of screenshots, CSVs, chat exports. OCR phone screenshots. Detect timezone and which days the shop was closed.

**Agent 2 — Identity stitcher.** Match name, phone, handle, and email across channels. Flag unmatched one-offs.

**Agent 3 — Intent miner.** Extract service asked, timing, budget hints. Never invent a price that is not on the menu note.

**Agent 4 — Ranker.** Score `hot / warm / ignore` against open calendar slots. Medical, legal, or safety-critical lines go to the human first.

**Agent 5 — Pack drafter.** Fill the table, five replies, capacity warning. Every cell cites a source or `not-in-record`. Refuse to send the replies.

**Human operator.** Strip gossip, fix names, refuse to message the lead. The model does not post in the shop's DMs.

Stack to start: coding + browser agents, OCR, one LLM, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. No product UI until the fifth paid job.

## Risks and mitigations

- Wrong ranking / missed emergency: human reviews every hot row; emergency-looking medical or leak threads get a phone call to the owner, not a silent PDF.
- Privacy: isolated processing, delete after 14 days unless they opt in. Redact kids and neighbors by default.
- Auto-reply temptation: v1 never posts. The owner pastes.
- Overlap with missed-call pack: if the input is one voicemail, send them to 2026-09-07. This pack starts at 8+ mixed inquiries from a closed period.
