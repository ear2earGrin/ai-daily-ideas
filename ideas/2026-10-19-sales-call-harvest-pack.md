## Concept

Sell a **same-week sales call harvest pack** when a solopreneur, freelance marketer, or small service business has recent sales or discovery calls and needs campaign assets before the momentum dies.

The customer uploads (or pastes):

- 1–5 call transcripts, notes, or recording links (with permission)
- a short description of the offer, price range, and who the buyer is
- optional: existing landing page, current ads, or brand voice note

Agents plus one human operator return within 48–72 hours:

1. An objection and pain ledger: ranked quotes and paraphrases tagged to source call + timestamp or note, with frequency.
2. A landing-page copy draft: hero, subhead, 3–5 benefit blocks, social-proof lines drawn only from the calls, and a primary CTA.
3. 5–10 ad angles / hooks: each with a one-line angle, suggested visual direction, and the call quote that supports it.
4. 3–5 ready-to-adapt outreach or email snippets that reuse the strongest language.
5. Source ledger: every claim or quote cites a call id or is marked “not in the provided materials.”

This is **not** full creative production, **not** ad account management, and **not** guaranteed results. It is a productized desk that turns hours of recordings into an inspectable campaign floor a solo or agent can act on the same week.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: solopreneurs, indie hackers, and freelance marketers (EU/UK/US) who run discovery or sales calls for their own offer or for clients and want to ship more experiments without hiring a copywriter or sitting in transcripts all weekend.
- Urgent pain: the calls happened, the objections are clear in the audio, but by the time they extract language the week is gone and the ads stay generic.
- Existing workaround: re-listen to the calls themselves, ask a general chatbot without citations, or skip the harvest and write from memory.

## Why this works

- Market signal: today’s posts frame “read last week’s sales calls then build the page and launch the ads” as the new default for a one-person marketing function. The raw material already exists in most service businesses; the missing piece is a cited, shippable extract.
- Agent advantage: ingest multiple transcripts, extract repeated pains and exact phrasing, draft structured copy, and keep every line traceable overnight.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has run campaigns can tighten voice, kill weak angles, and deliver a pack the buyer can hand to an ad agent or post themselves in under 45 minutes of review.

2. Demand test: post one anonymized sample pack on X and in 2–3 solopreneur/freelancer communities. DM 15 people who recently posted about sales calls, ad experiments, or one-person marketing teams. Offer the first 3 packs at 69.
3. Success bar: 4 serious replies and 2 paid packs in 14 days. Kill if 20 outbound touches produce zero deposits.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept transcripts, notes, or links. Normalize to text, assign call ids, flag low-quality or missing permission.

**Agent 2 — Extractor.** Pull repeated objections, pains, desired outcomes, and strong buyer phrases. Score by frequency and intensity. Cite source.

**Agent 3 — Copy drafter.** Produce the landing-page blocks and ad angles using only cited language. Mark any filler as “not from calls.”

**Agent 4 — Snippet writer.** Draft the outreach lines and the source ledger.

**Human operator.** Cut weak angles, fix voice, confirm no invented claims, and export PDF + Markdown. Do not contact the customer’s prospects.

Stack to start: existing coding/browser agents, one LLM, plain Markdown or python-docx, Stripe Payment Link, mailbox. No product UI until the fifth paid job.

## Risks and mitigations

- Invented claims or weak evidence: every line cites a provided call or is marked “not in the materials.” Operator rejects drafts that add testimonials or results not present.
- Confidential conversations: isolated processing, no training on client calls, delete after 30 days unless they opt in. Require confirmation that the customer has rights to the recordings.
- Overlap with generic copy services: the pack is specifically a cited extract from *their* calls, not a from-scratch landing page. Positioning and final send stay with the buyer.
- Buyer expects full campaign execution: deliver the pack and stop. Building the page, launching the ads, or reporting results is a separate engagement.
- Low-quality input: if transcripts are unusable, return a short note and partial refund rather than invent language.
