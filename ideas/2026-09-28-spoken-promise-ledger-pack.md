## Concept

Sell a **same-week spoken-promise ledger pack** when a solopreneur, freelancer, or 1-5 person shop already talks to clients on calls, WhatsApp, X DMs, and Slack — and the only memory is the owner's head.

The customer uploads or points at:

- last 7 days of call notes, transcripts, or voice-memo dumps they are willing to share
- Slack / email / WhatsApp / X DM exports for the same clients
- live CRM or Notion rows (or a CSV of deals / jobs)
- last invoices or proposals sent that week
- optional: one blow-up (client: "you promised Friday"; owner: "I never wrote that")

Agents plus one human operator return within 48 hours:

1. A promise floor: each extracted commitment tagged `in-record-written` / `spoken-only` / `denied-by-later-message` / `not-in-record`.
2. A ledger card per live job (max 8 in pack one): who promised what, channel, timestamp, whether it hit CRM / invoice / agent prompt, pause before send/spend.
3. A last-7-day receipt: promises that landed in a written system vs promises that only exist in a call. Numbers only from their files or marked `draft-unverified`.
4. A 7-line morning card: which promise is due today, which agent would invent it, what stays paused, what the human must confirm before 10:00.
5. A first-week script: write three spoken promises into the CRM tonight; pin one "before send/spend, show the plan" note; do not let an agent email a date that is only in a transcript.
6. A source appendix. No invented hours saved. No "we listened to every call."

This is **not** a call-recording SaaS, **not** a CRM, and **not** a 24/7 meeting bot. It is a productized desk for *one shop, one week of spoken work*, so "we promised it on the call" becomes a file.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US freelancers, indie product shops, and 1-5 person agencies who close work on Zoom/Meet and then let agents draft follow-ups.
- Urgent pain: a client cites a verbal date or discount; the invoice and the agent prompt disagree; the owner cannot prove what was said without scrubbing a 40-minute recording.
- Existing workaround: re-listen to the call, paste the transcript into ChatGPT, or eat the extra work.

## Why this works

- Market signal: this week's X feed named unwritten admin as the client-loss mechanism, agent babysitting as the hidden cost, invoice challenges as the moment context dies, and "AI drafts / owner decides" as the safe split. Existing catalog packs chase late invoices, log scope changes after they are already in chat, and write agent job charters — they do not reconstruct *spoken* commitments before the next send.
- Agent advantage: align messy transcripts to CRM rows and invoices; refuse to invent a promise that is not in the record.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has been burned by a verbal Friday can kill a false date in five minutes. Judgment about what counts as a promise is the product; the model is the clerk.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept transcripts, chat exports, CRM CSV, invoices. Detect one shop. Refuse a 40-client agency dump in pack one.

**Agent 2 — Promise extractor.** Pull dated commitments (dates, prices, scope extras, who does what). Tag spoken-only vs written. Never join their Zoom.

**Agent 3 — System matcher.** Compare each promise to CRM / invoice / agent prompt. Mark send/spend actions as human-review unless a written Approve exists.

**Agent 4 — Pack writer.** Promise floor, ledger cards, 7-day receipt, morning card, first-week script. Every number cites a file or `draft-unverified`.

**Human operator.** Kill fake promises, drop secrets, export PDF + Markdown. The model does not email the client and does not click Send.

Stack to start: coding + browser agents, one LLM, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. Exports and transcripts only in week one.

## Risks and mitigations

- Acting as their CRM admin: never log into their CRM. Draft the ledger. They paste the rows.
- Overlap with late-invoice chase (2026-09-07): that pack collects money already billed. This pack asks whether the bill matches what was spoken.
- Overlap with scope change-order (2026-09-04): that pack starts from a live job thread. This pack starts from *speech* that never reached the thread.
- Overlap with kickoff asset chase (2026-09-13): that pack hunts files. This pack hunts commitments.
- Overlap with judgment-rule harvest (2026-09-16): that pack extracts reusable rules. This pack lists this week's promises.
- Overlap with unread-inbox (2026-09-15): that pack is the unread pile. This pack is what was said before anything was written.
- Secret-stuffed transcripts: strip credentials and personal data before processing. Halt if live tokens appear.
- Consent: week one accepts owner-exported notes only. Do not scrape a client's private recording without the owner's file.
