## Concept

Sell a **same-day wrong-room card** when a freelancer or solo shop is about to let an agent send, or has just sent, a message that quotes a file, a CRM field, or another thread.

The customer sends:

- the draft or the sent message (email, WhatsApp, Slack, or a CRM note). A paste is enough.
- the intended recipient and the room it belongs to (this client, this matter, this person). One line.
- the file, field, or thread the agent quoted. A PDF page, a CRM field name, or a pasted excerpt.
- optional: a do-not-share list (the partner, the other lawyer, a second client, a surprise).
- optional: the other candidate field if the shop already knows the CRM has two dates.

Agents plus one human operator return within one business day:

1. A room line: who the fact was for, who it was addressed to, and whether those match. Tagged `same room`, `other room`, or `not-in-record`.
2. A source line: the number or name in the message next to the line it came from. If the PDF says 5 and the message says 50, tag `misread`. If the agent used a field the note does not name as trusted, tag `untrusted field`.
3. A share list: each named third party tagged `named in the send`, `on the do-not-share list`, or `not-in-record`. No guess about who "should" have been told.
4. A hold sentence the owner can paste above the send button: send, hold, or rewrite the one clause. Not a new email in the client's voice.
5. An undo note if the message already left: who to correct, what to retract, and what not to repeat in the correction.
6. A source appendix. No claim that the agent obeyed a permission toggle, that the CRM field is the finance date, or that the recipient did not already forward it.

This is **not** a confidentiality audit, **not** a CRM integration build, and **not** a Muse permission review. It is a productized desk that makes one send inspectable before the recipient answers.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US freelance operators and indie shops running one client-facing agent (inbox, WhatsApp, or a CRM reminder) for 1–5 person service businesses.
- Urgent pain: the message went out, the number was wrong, or a fact from matter A landed in matter B. The owner finds out when the venue, the lawyer, or the other client replies.
- Existing workaround: reread every draft by hand, or trust the permission toggle and hope the field the agent picked is the one finance trusts.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept a message paste, a recipient line, and a source excerpt. Refuse card numbers, national IDs, medical files, and full message archives.

**Agent 2 — Room clerk.** Compare the stated room to the addressee and to any named third party. Tag `same room`, `other room`, or `not-in-record`. Do not infer a secret from tone.

**Agent 3 — Source clerk.** Quote the number or name in the message and the line it came from. Tag `match`, `misread`, or `untrusted field`. If the paste has two dates and no trusted one, write `not-in-record`.

**Agent 4 — Hold writer.** Write send, hold, or rewrite-one-clause. Default to hold if the do-not-share list and the addressee intersect, or if the quantity does not match the source line.

**Agent 5 — Pack writer.** Room line, source line, share list, hold sentence, undo note, appendix.

**Human operator.** Delete invented rooms. Export Markdown + PDF. The model does not send the correction.

Stack to start: one LLM, a paste inbox, a Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. No CRM login and no Muse console in week one.

## Risks and mitigations

- Invented secrets: if the paste does not name the other lawyer or the surprise, do not guess. Write `not-in-record`.
- Pretending to audit permissions: the 8 Oct Muse story is a toggle that did not match the sync. This pack does not certify that an agent obeyed Full Disk Access or a CRM permission. It only reads the paste.
- Overlap with quiet-send check (2026-10-05): that pack catches a send that looked done and was not. This pack catches a send that did leave, to the wrong room or with the wrong line.
- Overlap with handoff-loss card (2026-10-04): that pack is facts that died between two summaries. This pack is a fact that survived and went to the wrong person.
- Overlap with false-explanation card (2026-10-06): that pack checks the story an agent told after a refund or cancellation. This pack checks the audience and the source line before or right after a send.
- Overlap with spoken-promise ledger (2026-09-28): that pack lists commitments from calls. This pack does not harvest promises. It only asks whether this message names a room it should not.
- Overlap with vendor renewal desk (2026-09-10): that pack catches a renewal notice already in the inbox. This pack is the reminder the agent is about to write from a field.
- Correction risk: the undo note retracts the clause. It does not repeat the secret in the correction.
- Channel terms: do not log into Gmail, WhatsApp, HubSpot, or Muse. The owner pastes.
