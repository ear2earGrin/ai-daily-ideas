## Concept

Sell a **same-night F1 Race Chaos Digest Pack** to solo content creators, newsletter operators, fantasy/sports-betting content accounts, and fan communities who need a cited, structured recap of the chaos (overtakes, lead changes, incidents, key battles) before the post-race cycle ends.

The customer points at (or the operator scrapes):

- official timing/results pages or public CSV/JSON exports

- optional: specific driver focus (e.g., "Ferrari collision deep-dive" or "Verstappen title challenge")

Agents plus one human return within a few hours:

1. Chaos scorecard: overtake density, lead changes, safety car impact, penalty cluster map (cited).
2. Key incident timeline with source links.
3. Ready-to-post thread outline or newsletter section (Markdown + suggested images/alt text).
4. One-paragraph "why this race felt different" insight.
5. Source appendix. No invented stats.

This is **not** a full race-prediction model or betting tipster. It is a productized research + writing desk that turns the public chaos numbers into a shippable creator asset the same evening.
