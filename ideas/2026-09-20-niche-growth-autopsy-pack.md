## Concept

Sell a **same-week niche growth autopsy pack** when a solopreneur, ecom operator, or freelancer wants to know why one brand is working *this week* and does not have a research team.

The customer names:

- one niche (ecom snack brand, local trade SaaS, newsletter, DTC accessory, etc.)
- one target brand (or "pick the fastest-rising name in this niche this week")
- optional: one competitor they already watch
- optional: whether they want audio + newsletter excerpt or brief-only

Agents plus one human operator return within 48 hours:

1. A cited growth autopsy (8-12 pages equivalent in Markdown): what is growing, the claimed why, the proven why, ad/creative volume if public, channel mix, founder/story hook, what is *not* in the record.
2. A steal-this-week list: three moves the buyer can copy in 7 days without the target brand's budget.
3. A do-not-copy list: theater metrics, unproven rumors, brand-specific moats.
4. Optional 10-14 minute monologue script + TTS audio (one voice, no fake interview guests).
5. A 180-word newsletter / X thread excerpt with source footnotes.
6. A source appendix. No invented ad spend. No "they are crushing organic" without a URL.

This is **not** a full competitor-intel firm, **not** a daily news show, and **not** a promise the buyer will hit the same growth curve. It is a productized desk that turns last week's public crumbs into one reusable brief + listen.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US ecom operators, indie hackers, and freelance GTM people who already listen to founder podcasts and still cannot answer "why is *that* brand working this month."
- Urgent pain: they screenshot threads, never write the synthesis, and ship nothing.
- Existing workaround: 3-hour ChatGPT dump with no sources, or paying a researcher $800 for a slide deck that arrives after the trend moved.

## Why this works

- Market signal: this week's X feed named the exact factory — research agent → monologue → TTS → host → list. The missing product is a *cited pack a second operator will pay for*, not another empty show.
- Agent advantage: parallel public-source sweep, separate claimed vs proven, refuse spend numbers without a filing or ad-library count.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has actually run ads can cut the fake-clever strategy section in 20 minutes.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Lock niche + brand + date window. Refuse two brands in week-one SKU unless they paid the contrast add-on.

**Agent 2 — Source sweep.** Public posts, ad libraries, filings, job posts, reviews, shipping pages, founder interviews. Tag every claim `proven` / `claimed` / `not-in-record`.

**Agent 3 — Autopsy writer.** Growth story, channel mix, steal-this-week, do-not-copy, appendix.

**Agent 4 — Audio.** Monologue script from the brief only. No extra claims. TTS + loudness pass.

**Human operator.** Kill unsourced spend numbers, strip any scraped customer PII, export Markdown + PDF + MP3. The model does not message the target brand and does not run ads for the buyer.

Stack to start: research-capable coding agents, one TTS voice, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox, optional Transistor/RSS later. Week one can deliver a file drop, not a hosted show.

## Risks and mitigations

- Fake certainty: if ad spend is not in a public library or filing, write `not-in-record`. Do not guess ROAS.
- Defamation / rumor: mark rumors as rumors; do not accuse founders of fraud from a reply guy.
- Overlap with 2026-05-18 niche trend report: that product is a recurring *category* bundle. This pack is one *brand week* plus optional audio. Keep SKUs separate.
- Overlap with cited signal pack (2026-09-05): that pack is outbound distribution ammo. This pack is a steal-the-playbook brief. Route "I need posts" buyers to 09-05.
- Scope creep into becoming their content studio: one brand, one monologue, one excerpt. Stop.
