## Concept

Sell a **same-week holiday gift-window floor pack** when a maker, Etsy shop, cafe, or 1-5 person local store has a real holiday spike (Mid-Autumn, harvest moon, autumn gifting, a pop-up with an end date) and currently answers “can this arrive by Sunday?” from memory.

The customer uploads or points at:

- live SKU list or shop URL plus what is actually in stock this week
- last 20 DMs / emails / Instagram comments that mention gift, ship, pickup, wrap, or a date
- hours, last-post / last-pickup cutoff, carrier pickup time if they have one
- optional: last year’s sold-out note or a pop-up flyer with dates

Agents plus one human operator return within 48 hours:

1. A gift-window map: `in-stock same-day pickup` / `made-to-order with cutoff` / `ship-only, miss-the-festival` / `not-in-record`.
2. A cutoff card: last order time for in-person, last ship time for courier, last wrap add-on time. Numbers only from their hours or marked `draft-unverified`.
3. A 12-line reply pack: three gift-by-date answers, three sold-out / delay answers, three pickup-hour answers, three “we do not discount mid-window” answers. Cite the SKU or hour they gave.
4. A do-not-promise list: dates, stock, and wrap options the record does not support.
5. A one-page Sunday/festival morning scorecard: what sold, what is left, which DMs are still open.
6. A source appendix. No invented carrier SLAs. No fake mooncake inventory.

This is **not** a Shopify theme, **not** a Meta ads campaign, and **not** a hosted chatbot on their Instagram. It is a productized desk for *one shop, one holiday window*.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US/JP/KR makers, resin/jewelry/food gift shops, cafes with seasonal hours, and Etsy solos who already take gift DMs and miss half of them after 21:00.
- Urgent pain: the festival or game-weekend is *this* week; they will either over-promise ship dates or leave money on seen.
- Existing workaround: copy last year’s caption, answer DMs at 1am, or close the shop and lose the window.

## Why this works

- Market signal: today’s X feed is full of dated pop-ups, moon SKUs, cafe hours-as-offer, and owner-trap posts about small decisions and unread hard messages.
- Agent advantage: cluster 20 messy DMs into four intent buckets; refuse to invent a DHL cutoff; draft replies a human will actually send.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has packed a last-minute gift box can write the do-not-promise list. Judgment about what cannot leave the bench tonight is the product; the model is the clerk.

3. Success bar: 5 serious replies and 2 paid packs in 14 days. Kill if 25 outbound touches produce zero deposits.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept SKUs + hours + DM export. Detect one shop and one window. Refuse a 200-SKU marketplace in one pack.

**Agent 2 — Window parser.** Tag each SKU and hour `visible-in-record` / `named-but-unexported` / `not-in-record`. Never log into Etsy or Instagram.

**Agent 3 — Intent splitter.** Cluster gift / ship / pickup / wrap / discount asks. Flag pricing exceptions as human-only unless a written rule exists.

**Agent 4 — Pack writer.** Gift-window map, cutoff card, 12-line replies, do-not-promise list, morning scorecard. Every date cites a source or `draft-unverified`.

**Human operator.** Kill fake carrier days, drop customer names from the public sample, export PDF + Markdown. The model does not send the DMs.

Stack to start: coding + browser agents, one LLM, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. Screenshots and CSV exports only in week one.

## Risks and mitigations

- Acting as their social manager: never send customer messages. Draft only.
- Overlap with game-night offer pack (2026-09-21): that pack is a sports-thread local offer. This pack is holiday *fulfillment* cutoffs and gift replies. Sell both; do not merge files.
- Overlap with weekend inquiry dump (2026-09-08): that pack is inbound volume after a long weekend. This pack is dated gift promises.
- Overlap with overnight ops floor (same day): that pack is spend and sleep. This pack is stock, wrap, and ship dates.
- Invented logistics: if they send no carrier pickup time, the cutoff card must say `not-in-record` in plain text.
- Scope creep into building a chatbot: deliver the floor. Stop.
