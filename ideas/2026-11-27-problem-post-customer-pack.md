## Concept

Sell a **same-week (or same-day) problem-post customer pack** when an indie hacker, solopreneur, or small-product builder has shipped something and needs the first real conversations instead of more features.

The customer pastes or uploads:

- a short product description or one-sentence offer
- 5–10 problem keywords, phrases, or example complaints the product solves
- preferred sources (X, specific subreddits, optional HN)
- optional: current landing page or positioning notes

Agents plus one human operator return within 24–72 hours:

1. A short list of 8–15 recent public posts (X or Reddit) where someone describes the exact pain, each with original link/quote, date, and a one-line fit reason.
2. For the strongest 5–8: a paste-ready reply that answers what they actually said (not a generic pitch) and a short personal DM draft.
3. A kill or deprioritize note on weak matches so the builder does not waste the week on low-fit threads.
4. A one-page “how to use this” note: approval rules, what not to send, and suggested next search tweaks.
5. An appendix of source IDs so every post and draft is checkable.

This is **not** automated posting or sending, **not** a full CRM, and **not** a guarantee of replies. It is a productized research-and-drafting desk that turns the product into an inspectable set of live problem posts the builder can act on the same week.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US/Canada indie hackers, solopreneurs, and 1-person product builders who have shipped a tool, landing page, or small SaaS and have zero or near-zero customers.
- Urgent pain: the agent (or the builder) can ship in a weekend; finding people who already said “I need exactly this” still takes hours of scrolling or generic cold outreach that gets ignored.
- Existing workaround: paste “find people who need my product” into a chatbot, scroll X manually, or buy a lead list that does not match the language of real complaints.

## Why this works

- Market signal: this week’s posts treat “shipped but zero customers” as the new default indie-hacker joke, and distribution as the remaining human bottleneck. Tools that surface live problem language and draft for approval are already being shown and discussed.
- Agent advantage: run parallel searches across X and Reddit, extract the original complaint language, score fit against the supplied product, and draft only from what the post actually said. Refuse to invent posts or contacts.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has done manual problem-post hunting can drop weak matches, tighten the reply tone, and ship a pack in under 40 minutes of review. Taste about what is actually a conversation starter is the product; the model is the search clerk.

## Monetization

- Primary model: per-pack service, plus a retainer for builders who want a fresh pack after they tweak positioning or ship a new feature.
- First price test: 89 EUR/USD for a standard pack (one product, 8–15 posts, 5–8 drafts); 129 for denser keywords or multi-source; 109 for same-day (4–8 h).
- Upsell: 39 “next-search tweak” after they send the first batch; 199/month retainer for up to two packs; 19 “voice-pass only” add-on.
- Fun / public variant: publish one anonymized sample pack (synthetic product + resulting posts and drafts) each week as marketing.

## Validation plan

1. Riskiest assumption: an indie builder will pay 89–129 for a cited problem-post pack instead of continuing to scroll or asking a free chatbot.
2. Demand test: post one anonymized sample pack on X and in 2–3 indie-hacker communities. DM 15 people who recently posted “zero customers,” “shipped but no users,” or “distribution is the hard part.” Offer the first 3 packs at 59.
3. Success bar: 4 serious replies and 2 paid packs in 14 days. Kill if 20 outbound touches produce zero deposits.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept product description, keywords, and sources. Flag overly broad keywords (“AI,” “productivity”).

**Agent 2 — Post finder.** Run X advanced search and Reddit queries. Return only recent, public posts that match the problem language. Cite every result.

**Agent 3 — Fit and draft writer.** Score fit, write the one-line reason, and produce reply + DM drafts only for strong matches. Refuse invented contact details.

**Agent 4 — Pack assembler.** Format the list, kill note, and “how to use” page. Every claim cites a source ID or is marked “inferred — confirm.”

**Human operator.** Remove invented posts, drop weak matches, tighten reply tone so it answers the actual complaint, and export Markdown + PDF. Do not post or send anything.

Stack to start: browser/search agents with X and Reddit access, one LLM, plain Markdown, Stripe Payment Link, mailbox. No product UI until the fifth paid job.

## First 7-day action plan

2. Build one public sample pack from a synthetic product (“weekend landing-page tool for freelancers”).

4. Send 15–20 outbound notes to recent “zero customers” / “distribution is hard” / “agent shipped the page” posts.
5. Run two paid pilots.
6. Time human review. Target under 40 minutes after job two.
7. Keep or kill.

## Risks and mitigations

- Invented posts or fake contacts: only public posts the search actually returns; every item cites a URL or post ID. Operator rejects drafts that invent threads.
- Over-claiming as automated distribution or guaranteed leads: deliver a research pack and drafts only. Disclaimer on every PDF: human must approve before any send.
- Thin or noisy results: if fewer than 5 clear matches appear, return a shorter pack and partial refund rather than pad with weak posts.
- Overlap with earlier radar or outreach packs: those surface general niche signals or pitch automation to local businesses; this pack is product-specific live problem language plus approval-ready conversation starters. Differentiate in sales copy.
- Buyer expects the operator to post or manage the replies: deliver the pack and stop. Live sending or follow-up management is a separate engagement.
