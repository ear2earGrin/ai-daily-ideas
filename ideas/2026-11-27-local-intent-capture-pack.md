# Local Intent Capture Pack for Home Service Businesses

**Date:** November 27, 2026

## Concept

Sell a **local intent capture pack** to home-service businesses (plumbers, electricians, HVAC, cleaners, locksmiths, landscapers, etc.).

The operator configures agents (powered by tools like Agent Reach or browser agents) to monitor public social and forum conversations in a defined city + service area for high-intent posts. Agents return a daily shortlist of posts plus draft replies written in the business’s voice. The human reviews and sends. The customer gets warm, local leads without spending hours scrolling or paying for expensive lead marketplaces.

This is **not** a full CRM, **not** automated posting that risks accounts, and **not** a general social media manager. It is a productized daily intent desk for one service + one geography.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: Owner-operators or 1–5 person home service companies in a specific city who already get some work from word-of-mouth or Google and want more inbound without ads or cold calling.
- Urgent pain: They know people post “anyone know a good plumber?” or “my AC just died” on local groups and Reddit, but they don’t have time to monitor consistently and their replies sound generic or late.
- Existing workaround: Occasional manual checks, paid lead sites (Angi, Thumbtack, etc.), or ignoring social entirely.

## Why this works

- Market signal: Agent tools that remove API costs for social reading are widely discussed; vertical agent services are actively recommended as solo-friendly businesses; public intent is abundant and under-monitored by small local operators.
- Agent advantage: Continuous monitoring + intent scoring + voice-matched drafting that a human cannot sustain daily.
- Solo-operator advantage: One person who understands local service sales can set the filters, review drafts quickly, and refine the voice. Judgment on which posts are real buyers (vs. ranting) is the product; the agents do the watching.

## Monetization

- Primary model: Productized service (setup + retainer).
- First price test: 149 EUR/USD setup + first 7 days of daily packs for one service and one city; then 199/month for ongoing daily monitoring + drafts (target 5–15 qualified posts).
- Upsell: Multi-service or neighboring city add-on (99); weekly performance summary + reply templates (49); white-label version for marketing agencies serving local businesses.

## Validation plan

1. Riskiest assumption: Local service owners will pay ~150–200/month for a daily shortlist of social intent + drafts instead of free manual checking or expensive lead platforms.
2. Demand test: Run the system for one pilot city/service for 7 days. Post anonymized examples on X and relevant local business groups. DM 20 service owners who have recently posted about needing more leads or social presence. Offer first 3 pilots at 99.
3. Success bar: 3 serious conversations and 1 paid pilot within 14 days. Kill or pivot if outreach produces zero interest.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Monitor.** Use Agent Reach (or browser agent) to search defined keywords + location filters across X, Reddit, and relevant forums. Collect recent posts.

**Agent 2 — Score & filter.** Rank by urgency, location match, buyer language, and recency. Drop low-intent or out-of-area posts.

**Agent 3 — Draft.** Generate 2–3 reply options matching the client’s voice and service area, with clear next step.

**Human operator.** Review daily pack (target <10 minutes), choose/send best replies, log outcomes, refine filters weekly.

Stack to start: Agent Reach or equivalent free social tools, one LLM for scoring/drafting, simple delivery (Notion/Airtable/email/Slack), Stripe payment link. No paid social APIs required for the monitoring layer.

## First 7-day action plan

1. Define one pilot niche + city and keyword list.
2. Set up monitoring agent and run it manually for 3 days to collect sample posts.
3. Create one public sample pack (anonymized).
4. Publish sample + offer on a simple one-pager and X.
5. Reach out to 20 local service businesses.
6. Run one paid or free pilot and measure replies → conversations.
7. Document time spent and refine.

## Risks and mitigations

- Account restrictions or platform changes: Prefer read-only public data; never automate posting from the client’s accounts without explicit review. Keep human in the send loop.
- Low volume in some cities/services: Start with higher-volume niches (plumbing, HVAC, cleaning) and larger cities; expand geography if needed.
- Overlap with generic social listening tools: Differentiation is the local + service-specific filtering, voice matching, and done-for-you daily pack rather than a raw dashboard.
- Compliance: Stick to public posts; no scraping of private groups without permission.
