# Same-Night Game-Thread Local Offer Pack

**Date:** September 21, 2026

- Creator-economy posts the same day still say the internet is up for grabs for one-person shops with agents, but distribution beats more tools. Local owners do not need another agent; they need copy that ships while the thread is still hot.
- Catalog already owns proof-clip farms (2026-09-12), cited signal packs (2026-09-05), and problem-intent replies (2026-09-09). It does not own a *same-night sports-thread → local offer* desk with a kill-time before kickoff-plus-six-hours.

## Concept

Sell a **same-night game-thread local offer pack** when a bar, restaurant, merch stall, gym, or one-person sports newsletter needs to post while a team or fight is trending and does not have a writer on staff.

The customer sends:

- city / venue / channel list (IG story, X, Google post, newsletter blurb)
- team or event they are allowed to mention (no unlicensed player likeness claims)
- hours, special they can actually honor tonight (wings, pint, late kitchen, watch-party table)
- optional: last 3 posts that performed, plus a 60-second voice note on tone

Agents plus one human return within 90 minutes of kickoff or within 3 hours of the trend appearing:

1. A 6-line cited thread brief: why this name is trending *today*, three source posts, one fact that will be stale by morning.
2. Three ready-to-paste posts (short / mid / story) that mention the venue special and the *event*, not a player's private life.
3. One offer card: price, hours, constraint ("while the kitchen is open"), and a line the staff can say out loud.
4. A do-not-say list: injuries, betting odds as advice, unverified fights, political pile-ons riding the same trend list.
5. A source appendix. If the trend is a rumor, the pack says `not-in-record` and ships a generic Good-Monday special instead.

This is **not** a sportsbook, **not** a rights-cleared highlight factory, and **not** a daily newsletter SaaS. It is a productized desk that turns a public trend into one night of local distribution.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: US/UK/EU independent bars, late kitchens, merch pop-ups, and one-person sports newsletters in NFL / UFC / college towns.
- Urgent pain: the city is talking about the Colts or UFC 331 and their last post is last Tuesday's lunch special.
- Existing workaround: steal a meme, post nothing, or wait for a freelancer tomorrow.

## Why this works

- Market signal: today's X homepage is almost entirely live sports names. Local operators lose the window every Monday.
- Agent advantage: scan trends, pull three primary posts, refuse rumor, draft channel-length copy.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has worked a Saturday night can reject a joke that will get a bartender in trouble. Judgment is the product.

## Monetization

- Primary model: per-pack service.
- First price test: 79 USD / 79 EUR for one night, one venue, three channels; 129 if they want a 250-word newsletter blurb plus the posts.
- Upsell: 249 for a 4-game Sunday/Monday retainer (one pack per window); 39 add-on for a staff cue card PDF.
- Fun variant: publish one synthetic Indianapolis kitchen pack on a Colts night as marketing. Never invent a score.

## Validation plan

1. Riskiest assumption: a bar manager will pay ~79 the same night instead of posting a stock photo.
2. Demand test: on the next NFL Sunday/Monday, DM 30 independently owned sports bars and one-person sports accounts in two cities. Offer first 5 packs at 49 / 90-minute SLA.
3. Success bar: 8 serious replies and 2 paid packs in 14 days. Kill if 30 touches produce zero deposits.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Trend intake.** Name the event, time window, and whether the trend is a result, a rumor, or a personality pile-on.

**Agent 2 — Citation clerk.** Pull three public posts and one box-score or official schedule fact. Tag `proven` vs `not-in-record`.

**Agent 3 — Copy desk.** Draft three posts + offer card + do-not-say list in the venue's voice.

**Human operator.** Kill rights problems, betting-advice language, and stale jokes. Send PDF + paste pack.

Stack: X search, one LLM, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. No official league partnership required in week one because the copy talks about the *event happening*, not licensed marks as a product.

## First 7-day action plan

1. Write the do-not-say standard (injuries, odds-as-advice, rumor, politics riding the sports list).
2. Build one public sample pack from tonight's Colts / Chargers / UFC window using only public facts.

## Risks and mitigations

- Trademark / publicity rights: never sell player-likeness merch copy. Event + venue + hours only unless the customer already owns the license.
- Fake scores: if the game is live and the source is a fan account, mark `not-in-record` and avoid the number.
- Overlap with proof-clip farm (2026-09-12): that pack mines the shop's own footage. This pack mines the public thread the same night.
- Overlap with cited signal pack (2026-09-05): that pack is weekly niche intelligence. This pack dies in six hours.
- Scope creep into becoming their social manager: three posts, one offer, one night. Stop.
