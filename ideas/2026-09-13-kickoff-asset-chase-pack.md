## Concept

Sell a **same-week kickoff asset chase pack** when a freelancer or tiny studio has a signed yes and an empty inbox of files.

The customer uploads:

- the yes-email / signed SOW / platform award
- the original brief or proposal
- whatever the client already sent (Drive link, Notion, Slack export, logo zip)
- optional: their standard intake form, tool list, and "cannot start without" rules

Agents plus one human operator return within 24 hours:

1. A kickoff card: job, start date, decision-maker, promised assets vs received assets.
2. A missing-asset matrix: brand kit, access (repo, ads, analytics, CMS, Stripe), voice samples, legal entities, stakeholders. Each row `received / partial / missing / never specified`.
3. A three-touch chase sequence the freelancer can paste: polite ask, specific list, blocked-until date.
4. A kickoff agenda (30 minutes) that only covers what is still open.
5. A "blocked until" note the freelancer can send instead of starting unpaid discovery.
6. A source appendix. Every missing line cites a brief clause, email timestamp, or `not-in-record`.

This is **not** a PM tool, **not** a password manager, and **not** the operator logging into the client's ads account. It is a clerk that turns "we start Monday" into a send-ready chase before the first unpaid week disappears.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US freelancers and 2-5 person studios in design, web, content, and automation who close 2-8 jobs a month and still burn week one waiting on files.
- Urgent pain: the client said yes on Thursday; Monday is "start"; the Drive is empty; the freelancer is about to invent a brand kit or work off a 2019 logo.
- Existing workaround: a 14-item Notion template nobody fills, a Slack nudge that gets a thumbs-up, or starting anyway and eating the rewrite.

## Why this works

- Market signal: this week's X feed treats idle agents and unanswered email as the small-business bottleneck. The catalog already owns disputes, change orders, and invoice chase. The *first unpaid week* is still unowned.
- Agent advantage: parse SOW + thread; extract implied assets ("you'll have access to GA4"); refuse to invent a login that was never promised.
- Solo-operator advantage: one operator who has started a job on a screenshot can tell a real blocker from a nice-to-have font file. Judgment is the product; the model is the clerk.

## Validation plan

1. Riskiest assumption: a freelancer will pay ~79 instead of sending one more "any update on the files?" Slack.
2. Demand test: post one anonymized sample pack on X and in 3 freelancer rooms. DM 25 people who just posted "client said yes" or "still waiting on brand assets." Offer the first 5 packs at 49 / 24h SLA.
3. Success bar: 5 serious replies and 2 paid packs in 14 days. Kill if 25 outbound touches produce zero deposits.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Normalize SOW, yes-email, and any zip. Detect start date, buyer, promised artifacts. Refuse to invent a start date that is only in a verbal note.

**Agent 2 — Asset extractor.** Pull implied and explicit assets from the brief (brand, access, data, legal, people). Tag each with source id.

**Agent 3 — Received matcher.** Compare the upload pile to the asset list. Mark received / partial / missing / never specified.

**Agent 4 — Chase drafter.** Write three-touch sequence, kickoff agenda, blocked-until note. Every line cites a source id.

**Human operator.** Kill invented logins, drop secrets that should stay in a password manager, export PDF + paste pack. The model does not email the client.

Stack to start: coding + browser agents, one LLM, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. No client-account login in week one. No sending on their behalf until the fifth paid job.

## Risks and mitigations

- Collecting passwords: never store live credentials. Ask for "access granted" proof (screenshot of invite), not the password.
- Overlap with scope change: if the client is adding work, send them to 2026-09-04. This pack starts from a *missing intake*, not a new deliverable.
- Overlap with late invoice: if the pain is payment, send them to 2026-09-07.
- Starting unpaid anyway: the blocked-until note is optional. Do not lecture the freelancer into a fight.
- Confidential brand files: isolated processing, delete after 30 days unless they opt in.
