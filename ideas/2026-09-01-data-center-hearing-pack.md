# Data-Center Hearing Pack Agent for Local Stakeholders

**Date:** September 1, 2026

## X signal (today)

Worldwide X today is split between entertainment drops (`#FELIX_SlowItDown`, `#LoveIslandUSA`, `#WWERaw`) and a quieter but persistent civic thread: **data centers**. Snaplytics lists "Data Centers" among worldwide trends; posts argue about power bills, water, local jobs, and whether communities should subsidize AI infrastructure. That is a better solo-agent wedge than another fan-content mill, and it is not already in this catalog.

## Concept

Sell a **done-in-48-hours hearing pack** whenever a town, county, or neighborhood is facing a new data-center proposal, expansion, or rate fight.

The operator (one person) plus agents produce:

1. Plain-language one-pager: what is being proposed, who owns it, timeline.
2. Cost map: electricity, water, tax abatements, promised jobs vs. comparable projects.
3. Sentiment digest from X, local Facebook groups, and news comments (quotes + source links).
4. Question list for the public hearing (for residents *and* for council members).
5. Counter-brief in two versions: "approve with conditions" and "delay / demand more data".
6. Slide deck + 90-second explainer script a resident can read at the mic.

This is a productized research service, not legal advice and not activism-as-a-service. The customer picks the framing; the pack is sourced and cited.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: neighborhood association chairs, small-town journalists, municipal staff, real-estate developers adjacent to a site, or civic groups who have 72 hours before a hearing and no research intern.
- Urgent pain: the developer PDF is 180 pages; the X thread is noise; nobody has a single document they can hand a council member.
- Existing workaround: frantic Google + ChatGPT summaries with no citations + copy-pasting Facebook comments.

## Why this works

- Market signal: data-center siting is a live political and utility issue in the US and elsewhere; X is already the rumor layer.
- Agent advantage: ingest filings, rate cases, news, and social posts overnight; extract comparable projects; keep a citation ledger.
- Solo-operator advantage: you do not need a newsroom. One human reviews sources, kills hallucinations, and ships a branded PDF.

## Monetization

- Primary model: per-pack service.
- First price test: $249 rush pack (48h) or $149 standard (5 days).
- Upsell: $99 update after the hearing; $750/month retainer for a journalist or advocacy shop covering multiple sites; $1,500 custom testimony coaching (script + Q&A only, no legal representation).
- Fun / public variant: publish a free redacted sample pack after each paid job to attract the next town.

## Validation plan

1. Riskiest assumption: someone with a hearing in the next 30 days will pay $149+ for a sourced brief instead of asking ChatGPT.
2. Demand test: find 10 upcoming hearings (city agendas + "data center" + county name). DM or email the clerk, a local reporter, and one neighborhood page with a 1-page sample and a 48-hour offer.
3. Success bar: 3 reply conversations and 1 paid or deposit-backed pack in 14 days. Kill or pivot if zero replies after 25 outbound touches.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Source collector.** Watch X (`data center`, `data centre`, county + project name), Google News RSS, municipal agenda PDFs, SEC/press releases. Store URLs + timestamps.

**Agent 2 — Filing reader.** OCR/PDF extract of site applications, fiscal impact notes, and utility interconnection mentions. Output structured facts only: MW, gallons, tax deal, jobs promised, construction start.

**Agent 3 — Comparables.** Pull 3 similar projects (same MW class or same state) and tabulate promised vs. reported outcomes.

**Agent 4 — Sentiment cluster.** Group public comments into jobs / rates / water / noise / property values. Keep 8 short verbatim quotes with links.

**Human operator.** Fact-check numbers, strip legal conclusions, write the two-page "what to ask", export PDF + slides.

Stack to start: X search + browser agents you already have, a PDF/OCR step, one LLM for drafting, Notion or a markdown repo as the case file, Typst or Google Docs for the pack. No custom SaaS until the third paid job.

## First 7-day action plan

1. Build a pack template (6 pages + slides) with dummy data from one real public proposal.
2. List 15 hearings or zoning items in the next 45 days.
3. Publish a public sample (one historical, uncontroversial site) on X and in the repo.
4. Send 25 outbound notes with the sample attached.
5. Run one paid or deposit pilot.
6. Time the human review. If review > 4 hours, shrink the pack.
7. Decide: productize the template or drop the idea.

## Risks and mitigations

- Hallucinated numbers: every figure must carry a source URL or "unverified — ask staff".
- Appearing partisan: ship dual framing by default; customer chooses which cover letter to use.
- Defamation / quoting X: paraphrase unless the quote is public and attributed; no private-group scraping.
- Legal line: never claim to be an attorney or engineer; label as research briefing.
