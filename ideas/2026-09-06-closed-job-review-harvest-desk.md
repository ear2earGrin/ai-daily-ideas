## Concept

Sell a **24-hour closed-job review harvest pack** when the invoice is paid and the seller is about to disappear into the next brief.

The customer (freelancer, studio, clinic, or local vendor) uploads:

- the closed brief / SOW / booking note
- the deliverable or visit summary (or a URL + export)
- the thread and the paid invoice
- optional: existing Google / Clutch / platform profile URL, past reviews, house voice notes

Agents plus one human operator return:

1. A close-out ledger: what was delivered, what was paid, what the buyer praised, what stayed messy.
2. A cited excerpt table: buyer praise or acceptance next to the artifact, or `not-in-record`.
3. A ask / wait / skip decision. Skip if the thread is already sour; do not farm a 2-star.
4. A three-touch ask the seller can paste: day 0 thank-you + one specific prompt, day 4 shorter nudge, day 10 archive. No fake urgency, no gift-for-review language.
5. A one-line review prompt the buyer can copy ("You helped us with X; the part that mattered was Y").
6. Two reply drafts if a review lands: public thank-you and a private follow-up if they mention a leftover issue.
7. A source appendix. Every claim points at a message timestamp, invoice line, or `not-in-record`.

This is **not** a review-bombing agency and **not** a reputation SaaS. It is a productized clerk that turns "I should ask them later" into one sendable file.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US freelancers, 2-8 person studios, clinics, and local vendors who close 4-20 jobs a month and have thin public proof.
- Urgent pain: the work is paid, the buyer said "thanks, looks good," and six weeks later the next proposal has zero recent reviews.
- Existing workaround: a guilty WhatsApp at midnight, a generic "pls review us" email, or doing nothing until a bad review arrives first.

## Why this works

- Market signal: this window's X feed names unrequested reviews as unpaid human labor sitting next to unchased invoices. Agent-economy threads assume reputation appears automatically after settlement. It does not.
- Agent advantage: parse threads and invoices; refuse to invent praise; emit an ask a human will actually send; keep the public-reply fence.
- Solo-operator advantage: one maker who has closed service work can tell a skip from an ask. Judgment is the product; the model is the clerk.

## Monetization

- Primary model: per-pack service.
- First price test: 49 EUR for jobs under 1,500 EUR; 89 EUR up to 6,000; 149 EUR above that. 24-hour SLA. Half price if they accept 3 days.
- Upsell: 19 EUR rush (8h); 29 EUR "review landed, draft the reply"; 15 EUR standing voice card; 199 EUR/month retainer for a shop that closes 8-15 jobs a month (includes 2 reply rounds).
- Fun / public variant: publish one synthetic or consented anonymized pack a week (close-out + three-touch ask + reply drafts) as X content.

## Validation plan

1. Riskiest assumption: a seller will pay 49-89 EUR for a cited ask pack instead of sending one awkward "pls review" message.
2. Demand test: post one anonymized sample pack on X and in 3 freelancer or local-vendor communities. DM 20 people who just posted "client paid" or "wrapped a project." Offer the first 5 packs at 39 EUR / 24h.
3. Success bar: 5 serious replies and 2 paid packs in 14 days. Kill if 25 outbound touches produce zero deposits.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept PDF/DOCX/email/export/profile URL. Detect job type and platform (Google, Clutch, Upwork, direct).

**Agent 2 — Close-out parser.** Extract what shipped, what was paid, remaining open items.

**Agent 3 — Praise miner.** Pull buyer acceptance and specific compliments. Never invent a quote.

**Agent 4 — Ask classifier.** Tag `ask / wait / skip`. Sour threads skip. Partial refunds wait.

**Agent 5 — Pack drafter.** Fill ledger, three-touch sequence, review prompt, and reply drafts. Every cell cites a source id or `not-in-record`.

**Human operator.** Delete gift-for-review language, keep the voice sendable, refuse to post anything public. The model does not message the buyer.

Stack to start: coding + browser agents, python-docx / pypdf, one LLM, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox or Notion inbox. No product UI until the fifth paid job. Google posting APIs are out of scope for week one.

## Risks and mitigations

- Review gating / incentive rules (Google, platform ToS): never offer value in exchange for a review. Operator strips that language.
- Farming unhappy buyers: classifier defaults to skip when the thread is cold or disputed. Point disputes to the 2026-09-03 verdict desk.
- One-sided record: label packs "based on materials from the seller only."
- Confidential files: isolated processing, no training on client work, delete after 30 days unless they opt in.
- Overlap with revival desk: if the quote was never accepted, refuse this SKU and send them to 2026-09-05.
