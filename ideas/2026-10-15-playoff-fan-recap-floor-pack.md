# Playoff Fan Recap & Engagement Floor Pack for Local Sports Bars and Solo Creators

**Date:** October 15, 2026

The catalog has general content and ops packs but nothing that owns the same-day sports playoff recap + fan-reply window for bars and creators.

## Concept

Sell a **same-day playoff fan recap & engagement floor pack** when a local sports bar, solo sports creator, or fan page has a live game window and currently answers “what just happened?” from memory or posts generic highlights.

The customer shares or points at:

- the game (public scoreboard URL or simple ID)
- last 10–20 relevant public X posts or a short search they care about
- house rules / tone (e.g. “no betting advice”, “highlight local fans”, “max 3 stats”, preferred hashtags or logo notes)
- optional: existing brand voice samples

Agents plus one human operator return within a few hours of the final out (or next morning):

1. A cited short X/thread recap (key plays + sources).
2. A longer bar-TV or short-form script outline.
3. 8–12 ready-to-adapt reply templates for common fan comments.
4. A do-not-say list (anything not supported by the provided sources).
5. Optional simple video storyboard or Remotion-style prompt.
6. Source appendix. No invented advanced stats. No betting advice unless explicitly requested and clearly labeled.

This is **not** a full media agency, **not** a betting service, and **not** an automated posting bot. It is a productized desk for *one game / one window*.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: local sports bars, solo sports Twitter/TikTok/YouTube creators, small fan pages, and community accounts that already post during games.
- Urgent pain: the game ends late; they either post late, invent color, or stay silent and lose the engagement window.
- Existing workaround: copy ESPN highlights, post from memory, or ignore.

## Why this works

- Market signal: today’s X feed is full of playoff names and real-time fan chatter. Free agent tools exist for video and data, but small operators still do the last mile by hand.
- Agent advantage: pull public scores and threads, cluster comments, draft cited recaps and replies, refuse to invent stats.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has watched a playoff series can enforce “no invented color” and local tone in minutes. Judgment about what a bar can actually say is the product; the model is the clerk.

## Monetization

- Primary model: per-pack service.
- First price test: 79–99 USD/EUR for one game window (recap + replies); 149 if video script/storyboard is included.
- Upsell: multi-game retainer for the rest of the series; 39 rush; 29 “local fan shout-out” add-on.
- Fun / public variant: publish one synthetic Dodgers–Brewers sample pack each day of the series as marketing. Never use real customer rules or private data.

## Validation plan

1. Riskiest assumption: a local bar or solo creator will pay ~79–149 for a cited same-day pack instead of posting from memory.
2. Demand test: post one sample pack on X and in sports-bar / creator communities. DM 15 people who posted during the trending games. Offer the first 3 packs at 59.
3. Success bar: 4 serious replies and 2 paid packs in 14 days. Kill if 20 outbound touches produce zero deposits.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept game ID/URL, public threads, and house rules. Flag missing tone or rules.

**Agent 2 — Source collector.** Pull public scores and relevant posts. Tag every claim `visible-in-record` / `draft-unverified`.

**Agent 3 — Recap & reply writer.** Draft the thread, script outline, and reply pack. Cite or label every number.

**Agent 4 — Pack assembler.** Produce do-not-say list and source appendix.

**Human operator.** Kill invented stats, adjust local tone, export Markdown/PDF + ready-to-copy text. Do not post as the client.

Stack to start: browser + research agents, one LLM, public scoreboards and X search, Stripe Payment Link, mailbox. Screenshots and public links only in week one.

## First 7-day action plan

1. Write a default evidence standard (citation rules, no-invent-stats rule, no-betting default).
2. Build one public sample pack from a synthetic playoff night.
3. Publish sample + prices + “we never post as you” disclaimer on a one-pager and X.
4. Send 15–20 outbound notes to recent playoff posters (bars and creators).
5. Run two paid pilots.
6. Time human review. Target under 30 minutes after job two.
7. Keep or kill.

## Risks and mitigations

- Invented stats or color: every number must cite a public source or be marked draft-unverified. Operator rejects drafts that invent.
- Betting or advice creep: default no betting language; only add if client explicitly requests and it is labeled.
- Overlap with general content packs: this pack is dated to one game window and sports-specific sources.
- Scope creep into full social management: deliver the pack and stop. Posting is the client’s job.
- Low perceived urgency: lead with “post while the thread is still hot” and the concrete do-not-say list.
