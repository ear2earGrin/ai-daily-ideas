## Concept

Sell a **same-week (or same-day) local SMB agent-automation outreach pack** when a solopreneur, freelancer, or boutique agency wants to sell websites, AI agents, or simple automation to local small businesses.

The customer pastes or uploads:

- target city or region and niche (e.g., “dentists in Austin” or “plumbing companies in Manchester”)
- optional criteria (no website, outdated site, no online booking, high review volume)
- preferred offer type (missed-call booking agent, review responder, simple lead form + follow-up, website refresh)
- optional: voice or brand notes for the messages

Agents plus one human operator return within 24–72 hours:

1. A researched list of 10–40 businesses with public source IDs (Maps, site, directory).
2. A one-line status for each (no site / old site / no booking / etc.).
3. A short, specific offer paragraph tailored to the observed gap.
4. Personalized cold email or DM drafts (subject + body) ready to send.
5. A tiny mock sketch or workflow diagram description for the offer.
6. An appendix of source IDs so every claim is checkable.

This is **not** a full website build, **not** live campaign management, and **not** a guarantee of replies. It is a productized research-and-writing desk that turns public local data into an inspectable outreach pack the operator can send today.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US/Canada solopreneurs, indie hackers, and 1–3 person service shops (web, automation, marketing, ops) who already use agents and want a repeatable way to find and pitch local SMBs.
- Urgent pain: manually researching and writing personalized outreach takes evenings; generic templates get ignored; they know local businesses need automation but lack a clean pack to start conversations.
- Existing workaround: hours on Google Maps + ChatGPT drafts that still need heavy editing, or buying generic lead lists.

## Why this works

- Market signal: this week’s posts treat multi-agent systems and local automation offers as practical ways for one person to generate revenue. The research-to-personalized-message step remains manual.
- Agent advantage: pull public business data, flag gaps, align every claim to a source ID, draft specific offers without inventing facts, and produce consistent message variants.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has sold similar services can tighten the offer language, remove weak businesses, and ship a pack in under 40 minutes of review.

## Monetization

- Primary model: per-pack service, plus a retainer for operators who want a fresh pack every week or month.
- First price test: 99 EUR/USD for a standard pack (10–15 businesses, one city/niche); 179 for 25–40 businesses; 149 for same-day (4–8 h).
- Upsell: 29 “follow-up message round” after first replies; 249/month retainer for up to three packs; 19 “brand-voice pass” add-on.
- Fun / public variant: publish one anonymized sample pack (synthetic city + resulting outreach) each week as marketing.

## Validation plan

1. Riskiest assumption: a solo operator will pay 99–179 for a cited local outreach pack instead of spending another evening researching and writing messages.
2. Demand test: post one anonymized sample pack on X and in 2–3 indie-hacker / freelance communities. DM 15 people who recently posted about local lead gen, automation agencies, or “scanning Maps for clients.” Offer the first 3 packs at 69.
3. Success bar: 4 serious replies and 2 paid packs in 14 days. Kill if 20 outbound touches produce zero deposits.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept city/niche, criteria, and offer type. Normalize and flag missing detail.

**Agent 2 — Research.** Pull public listings, assign source IDs, flag website/booking gaps. Refuse to invent contact details or private data.

**Agent 3 — Offer drafter.** Write a short, specific automation or agent offer tied to the observed gap. Cite sources; mark anything inferred.

**Agent 4 — Outreach writer.** Produce subject lines, message bodies, and a simple mock description. Keep claims checkable.

**Human operator.** Remove invented facts, drop weak leads, tighten voice, and export Markdown + PDF. Do not send the messages or manage replies.

Stack to start: browser/search agents, one LLM, plain Markdown, Stripe Payment Link, mailbox. No product UI until the fifth paid job.

## Risks and mitigations

- Invented contact data or private details: only public sources; every claim cites a source ID or is marked “inferred — confirm.” Operator rejects drafts that invent emails or phone numbers.
- Spam / compliance: deliver the pack only; the customer is responsible for sending and any local regulations. Include a short disclaimer.
- Thin results: if the niche yields fewer than the promised number of clear gaps, return a shorter pack and partial refund rather than pad with weak leads.
- Overlap with earlier radar or directory packs: those are broader opportunity briefs; this pack is specifically local SMB research + sendable automation outreach. Differentiate in sales copy.
- Buyer expects the operator to send the messages or close deals: deliver the pack and stop. Live outreach or sales calls are a separate engagement.
