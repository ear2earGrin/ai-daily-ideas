## Concept

Sell a **same-week overnight ops floor pack** when a solopreneur, freelancer, or 1-5 person shop wants agents (or a VA-shaped agent) to work while they sleep — after at least one real customer has paid — and they currently have a verbal "don't spend too much" rule.

The customer uploads or points at:

- the morning checklist a human or VA already runs (notes, Loom, Notion, Slack canvas)
- last 14 days of ad / Shopify / Stripe / inbox spend exports they are willing to share
- tool list: storefront, ads, email, support, inventory, booking
- optional: one competitor or prior agent run that burned budget

Agents plus one human operator return within 48 hours:

1. A sleep-window map: tasks tagged `read-only overnight` / `write with cap` / `human-only until morning` / `not-in-record`.
2. A money-visibility scorecard: which tools the agent can actually *see* (payouts, ad spend, COGS, refunds, unpaid invoices) vs which are blind. Cite the export or `not-in-record`.
3. A kill-policy card: one daily spend ceiling, one SKU/test kill rule, one "stop if refund rate / bounce / 5xx" tripwire. Numbers only from their exports or marked `draft-unverified`.
4. A 7-line morning scorecard the owner can read in four minutes: what ran, what spent, what died, what needs a human taste call.
5. A first-night script: the three actions the agent may take tonight and the three it must queue.
6. A source appendix. No invented ROAS. No "set it and forget it."

This is **not** a hosted agent that spends their ad budget, **not** a Shopify app, and **not** an on-chain escrow court. It is a productized desk for *one shop, one sleep window*, so overnight work has a floor instead of a vibe.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US indie sellers, Shopify / Etsy / info-product solos, and freelance shops who already have one paid customer and a morning ritual a VA or ChatGPT half-runs.
- Urgent pain: they want overnight leverage after the first sale, but last week's agent or intern silently raised spend, restocked a dead SKU, or never saw that payouts were late.
- Existing workaround: leave ads on 24/7 with a gut cap, hire a VA who ghosts, or paste the store URL into an agent and hope it "just monitors."

## Why this works

- Market signal: this week's X feed named morning scorecards, $50/day kill policies, money-blind agents, overnight trust after first payment, and the un-automatable VA morning loop. Existing catalog packs cover approvals, spend invoices, and probation — not the sleep window itself.
- Agent advantage: parse messy checklists and CSV exports; refuse to invent a spend cap; draft a scorecard a human will actually open at 8am.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has watched an agent restock a loser SKU can write the three-action night script. Judgment about what must stay dark until morning is the product; the model is the clerk.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept checklist + exports + tool list. Detect one shop. Refuse to floor a 40-SKU marketplace in one pack.

**Agent 2 — Money-visibility parser.** Read the CSVs they actually uploaded. Tag each money surface `visible-in-export` / `named-but-unexported` / `not-in-record`. Never log into ads or Shopify.

**Agent 3 — Checklist splitter.** Turn the morning VA ritual into overnight vs morning-human buckets. Flag taste, refunds, pricing, and payouts as human-only unless the record shows a written rule.

**Agent 4 — Pack writer.** Sleep-window map, kill-policy card, 7-line scorecard, first-night script. Every number cites a row or `draft-unverified`.

**Human operator.** Kill fake ROAS, drop account secrets, export PDF + Markdown. The model does not connect to their ad account and does not flip campaigns.

Stack to start: coding + browser agents, one LLM, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. Exports only in week one.

## Risks and mitigations

- Acting as their media buyer: never connect to ads. Draft the cap. They paste it.
- Overlap with spend attribution (2026-09-21): that pack explains *last month's* invoice lines. This pack sets *tonight's* ceiling and morning card. Sell both; do not merge the files.
- Overlap with approval-lane (2026-09-19): that pack is who may write. This pack is what may run while they sleep.
- Overlap with exception-control (2026-09-15): that pack is runtime faults and cost spikes. This pack is the planned night floor even when nothing faults.
- Overlap with probation (2026-09-23): that pack onboards a new agent to one write surface. This pack assumes the shop already wants overnight work.
- Money-blind exports: if they send no payouts file, the scorecard must say `agents cannot see money` in plain text, echoing this week's sim report.
- Scope creep into building the agent: deliver the floor. Stop.
