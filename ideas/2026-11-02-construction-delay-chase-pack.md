## Concept

Sell a **same-week construction site delay & subcontractor chase pack** when a small general contractor, specialty sub, or 1–5 person shop is running one or more active projects and currently spends evenings piecing together emails, photos, and schedule updates to see what is late and who needs a nudge.

The customer uploads or points at (no logins required):

- recent project emails / WhatsApp / SMS exports or screenshots involving the sub or supplier
- schedule or Gantt export (CSV, PDF, or photo)
- site photos or daily logs
- optional: drawings or spec snippets, material order lists, prior chase messages

Agents plus one human operator return within 48–72 hours:

1. Delay signal ledger: each open item tagged with evidence (email date, photo timestamp, schedule line), current status, and citation.
2. Ready-to-send chase drafts: 3–5 short messages (email/SMS tone) for the top overdue subs or suppliers, with the specific missing item or late milestone cited.
3. Material-order and drawing-vs-spec flags: mismatches or missing deliveries marked `visible-in-record` or `not-in-record`.
4. One-page morning brief: what is already late, what the owner should chase today, what can wait.
5. Source appendix. No invented completion percentages or cost overruns.

This is **not** a full project-management SaaS, **not** live site monitoring cameras, **not** a legal claims package, and **not** an agent that emails the subcontractor on the buyer's behalf without approval. It is a productized desk that turns the evening pile of site inputs into a short, cited chase list the owner can act on before 8am.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: small GCs, specialty subcontractors (electrical, HVAC, concrete, finish), and freelance project managers in EU/UK/US/Canada running 1–5 concurrent jobs.
- Urgent pain: evenings spent scrolling chats and photos to figure out which sub is late, which material is missing, and whether the drawing matches what is being built; money and schedule slip while the owner is still on site.
- Existing workaround: sticky notes, late-night emails, a junior who ghosts, or ignoring the delay until the client calls.

## Why this works

- Market signal: today's explicit callout that construction is behind on agents for exactly these loops (delay tracking, sub chase, drawing checks, material orders). Persistent agent teams are trending, but the product is the cited pack that removes the evening interpretation work, not another always-on bot.
- Agent advantage: parse messy multi-source inputs (photos + emails + CSVs), extract delay evidence without inventing numbers, and draft concise chase language overnight.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has watched a sub miss a milestone can kill vague "please update" drafts, tighten the citations, and deliver a pack the buyer can send in under 30 minutes of review.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept emails, photos, schedule exports, and optional drawings. Normalize, flag missing dates or unreadable images.

**Agent 2 — Delay extractor.** Build the signal ledger with citations. Tag each item `evidence-backed` or `not-in-record`.

**Agent 3 — Chase drafter.** Write the short messages and the morning brief. Keep language factual and non-accusatory.

**Agent 4 — Flag writer.** Surface material and drawing mismatches only where the record supports them.

**Human operator.** Remove invented dates or blame, confirm every claim cites the provided materials, drop any personal contact data from the public sample, and export Markdown + PDF. Do not contact the subcontractor or log into the buyer's project software.

Stack to start: existing coding/browser agents, one LLM, plain Markdown, Stripe Payment Link, mailbox. No product UI until the fifth paid job.

## Risks and mitigations

- Invented delays or costs: every item cites provided materials or is marked "not in record." Operator rejects drafts that invent percentages or blame.
- Confidential project data: isolated processing, no training on client materials, delete after 30 days unless they opt in. No real addresses in samples.
- Overlap with general overnight ops or completion-receipt packs: this pack is specifically the construction evening loop of delay signals + ready chase drafts from site inputs, not a general agent floor or a receipt for agent side effects. Implementation stays with the buyer.
- Buyer expects the agent to email the sub or manage the schedule live: deliver the pack and stop. Live monitoring or automated sends are a separate engagement.
- Thin materials: if the customer has only a vague "the job is late" and no emails or photos, return a short gap list and partial refund rather than invent a ledger.
