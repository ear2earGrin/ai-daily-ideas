## Concept

Sell a **same-week NISSEF readiness pack** to Bulgarian accountants, bookkeepers, and 5–30 person firms that still send PDF or paper invoices and have heard "2028" without a file they can show a client.

The customer uploads:

- 3–10 recent outgoing invoices (PDF or export), with buyer names redacted if they want
- the accounting or invoicing tool name (Microinvest, Ajur, Business Navigator, Excel, a foreign SaaS, or "the accountant does it")
- VAT status, whether they invoice the state, and whether any buyer already demands an e-invoice
- optional: a one-paragraph note on what their software vendor has told them

Agents plus one human operator return within five business days:

1. A current-state card: PDF, spreadsheet, local software, or already structured. What fields are present, missing, or only in the PDF footer.
2. A draft-gap table against the published consultation points only: structured B2B/B2G format, real-time NISSEF reporting, NRA validation code, pre-filled VAT. Each row is `present`, `partial`, `missing`, or `not-in-record`. No row claims the law is final.
3. A vendor question list the accountant can forward: five questions for the software supplier, written so a non-lawyer can send them.
4. A 90-day action list: what to do before the consultation closes (23 Oct 2026), what to ignore until the draft is adopted, and what not to buy yet.
5. A client-forward note in Bulgarian and English, labeled as a readiness brief, not a legal opinion and not a filing.
6. A source appendix. Every requirement cites the consultation summary the operator used, or `not-in-record`.

This is **not** a law-firm opinion, **not** an NRA filing, and **not** a promise that 1 January 2028 is locked. It is a productized clerk that turns "we heard about e-invoicing" into a file an accountant can attach to a client email this week.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: Sofia and other BG accountants with 15–80 SME clients, plus owners of product or service firms who invoice in Bulgaria and still export PDFs.
- Urgent pain: the consultation closes 23 October 2026. Clients will ask "are we fine?" The accountant has a vendor slide, not a gap list tied to the client's actual invoices.
- Existing workaround: wait for the software vendor, or paste the draft into a chat model and forward an uncited answer.

## Why this works

- Market signal: the 30 Sep Storecove post is a dated public consultation, not a vibe. DAC8 reporting passing in September is the adjacent proof that NRA structured-data rules are moving, not theoretical.
- Agent advantage: extract invoice fields, diff them against a fixed checklist, and refuse to invent a legal duty the consultation text does not state.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person in Sofia who can read a Bulgarian draft and a PDF invoice can sell this without a team. The model is the clerk. The operator decides what is safe to put in the client note.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept PDF/export plus tool name. Refuse national IDs, bank secrets, and full customer lists. Redact buyer names in the output by default.

**Agent 2 — Invoice clerk.** Pull supplier, date, currency, VAT lines, buyer id if present, and whether the file is a PDF image or structured export. Quote the field or write `not-in-record`.

**Agent 3 — Draft diff.** Compare only against a pinned checklist taken from the public consultation summary. Tag `present`, `partial`, `missing`, `not-in-record`. Never upgrade a proposal into "the law requires."

**Agent 4 — Vendor questions.** Write five questions the accountant can send the software supplier. No invented product claims.

**Agent 5 — Pack writer.** Current-state card, gap table, 90-day list, bilingual client note, appendix.

**Human operator.** Read the Bulgarian client note. Delete any sentence that sounds like a legal opinion or a filing instruction. Export Markdown + PDF. The model does not email the NRA or the client.

Stack to start: one LLM, pypdf, a paste inbox, a Stripe or bank Payment Link, a mailbox. No NISSEF API in week one. Pin the consultation source URL and the date you read it in the appendix.

## Risks and mitigations

- Unauthorized legal practice: label every pack "readiness brief on files you sent, not a legal opinion and not a filing." The operator does not submit comments to the consultation unless the client asks in writing and the operator is acting in their own professional capacity.
- Draft changes: date-stamp the checklist. Sell the update as a separate 150 EUR pack.
- Overlap with the VAT packet desk (2026-09-28): that pack is cross-border OSS/IOSS evidence. This pack is domestic BG e-invoicing readiness against the NISSEF draft.
- Overlap with late-invoice chase (2026-09-07): that pack chases an unpaid invoice. This pack asks whether the invoice format will still be acceptable.
- Invented vendor support: if the upload does not include a vendor statement, the row is `not-in-record`.
- Confidential invoices: isolated processing, no training on client files, delete after 30 days unless they opt in.
