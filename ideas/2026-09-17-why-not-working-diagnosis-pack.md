# Same-Week Why-It's-Not-Working Diagnosis Pack for Solo Shops

**Date:** September 17, 2026

- Ajit (@Anvayance, 16 Sep, 2.5k views on adjacent agent-economy threads this week): an agent with a token budget starts cold-emailing to fund its own compute. Operators read this as survival loops, not strategy. The loop ships activity. It does not pick a better offer.

This catalog already owns unread-inbox floors (2026-09-15), agent exception + cost control (2026-09-15), vendor renewal notices (2026-09-10), judgment-rule harvests (2026-09-16), and inference receipts (2026-09-09). It does not own a *same-week diagnosis pack* that tells the solo which lever to change when the agents are busy and the bank is not.

## Concept

Sell a **same-week why-it's-not-working diagnosis pack** when a solopreneur, freelancer, or 1-5 person shop has automations, ads, or an "AI employee" running and still cannot name the broken step.

The customer uploads or exports:

- last 7-30 days of bank / Stripe / payout CSV (or screenshots if that is all they have)
- ad or outbound spend notes (Meta, Google, X, cold email tool, or "I posted and hoped")
- CRM / inbox / calendar snapshot: leads in, calls booked, jobs closed, refunds
- optional: agent traces, n8n run log, model invoice, one 10-minute voice note on "what I think is wrong"

Agents plus one human operator return within 48 hours:

1. A one-page funnel map: spend → lead → reply → book → close → cash, with the first broken step circled.
2. A cited number ledger: every claim points at a CSV row, ad screenshot, thread stamp, or `not-in-record`.
3. Three change options only (kill / raise / stop-doing), each with a 7-day test and a kill-switch.
4. A "do not touch" list so they do not rewrite the whole stack.
5. A 14-day operator checklist the human can run without a new SaaS login.
6. A source appendix. No invented CAC, no fake industry benchmarks dressed as their numbers.

This is **not** a growth agency, **not** bookkeeping, and **not** an always-on AI CEO. It is a productized diagnosis that makes the next change inspectable.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US solopreneurs, indie hackers, freelancers, and tiny local shops spending $200-3,000/month on ads + tools + model bills, with agents or automations already live.
- Urgent pain: activity is up (replies drafted, calls logged, posts shipped) and cash or close-rate is flat or down. The owner cannot tell if the offer, the channel, the follow-up, or the fulfillment is the hole.
- Existing workaround: another dashboard, a ChatGPT dump of the CSV, or "run more ads."

## Why this works

- Market signal: this week's X feed named the gap in plain language. AI-employee products sell motion. The experiment that used real bank accounts said diagnosis is still human.
- Agent advantage: stitch messy CSVs, ad screenshots, and traces; refuse to invent a CAC when the export is incomplete; keep the output to three tests.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has run a small shop can tell a real leak from a vanity metric. Judgment about the funnel is the product; the model is the clerk.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept zip/CSV/PDF/PNG/voice. Detect currency and date range. Refuse to merge two businesses in one pack.

**Agent 2 — Funnel miner.** Count spend, inbound, replies, books, closes, refunds, payouts. Flag missing stages as `not-in-record`.

**Agent 3 — Leak ranker.** Rank the first broken step. Draft three change options with a 7-day test each. Ban "post more" unless the record shows zero distribution.

**Agent 4 — Pack writer.** One-page map, number ledger, do-not-touch list, 14-day checklist. Every cell has a source id or `not-in-record`.

**Human operator.** Kill cute advice the shop cannot execute this week, drop client names from the public sample path, export PDF + Markdown. The model does not pause ads and does not touch the customer's bank.

Stack to start: coding agents, one transcriber, one LLM, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. No live ad-account OAuth in week one.

## Risks and mitigations

- Fake certainty: if the export has no ad costs, do not invent CAC. Circle the missing stage.
- Confidential money files: isolated processing, redaction pass, delete after 30 days unless they opt into the retainer.
- Overlap with exception-control: that pack reads agent *failures and model bills*. This pack reads *the business funnel*. If they only have traces, route them to 2026-09-15.
- Overlap with unread-inbox floor: that pack clears a queue. This pack says whether the queue is even the bottleneck. Sell both; do not merge the files.
- Scope creep into coaching: do not become their weekly therapist. Deliver three tests. Stop.
- Bad change executed: tests are 7 days with a kill-switch. The pack does not auto-pause campaigns.
