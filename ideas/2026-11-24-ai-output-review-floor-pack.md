## Concept

Sell a **same-week (or same-day) AI output review floor pack** when a solopreneur, freelancer, or SMB owner is drowning in AI drafts that still need checking against their own standards.

The customer pastes or uploads:

- 3–15 past approved examples (posts, emails, quotes, SOWs, captions, etc.)
- brand notes, voice guidelines, or a short “never do this” list
- a batch of recent AI drafts (or a link to a folder/export)
- optional: risk categories they already care about (legal claims, pricing, client names, tone)

Agents plus one human operator return within 24–72 hours:

1. A risk-tier table (low / medium / high) with clear criteria tied to the supplied examples.
2. A short reusable checklist per tier that a human can apply in under two minutes.
3. Annotated samples: 3–8 drafts with inline comments showing where they match or miss the bar, each claim cited to a source example or marked “inferred — confirm.”
4. A one-page “what we keep fixing” pattern note so the operator can update the prompt or data next cycle.
5. Paste-ready approval language for the high-risk items.
6. An appendix of source IDs so every judgment is checkable.

This is **not** full content production, **not** legal review, and **not** a guarantee that the drafts are error-free. It is a productized research-and-checklist desk that turns the client’s own materials into an inspectable review floor the owner or operator can use the same week.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US/Canada solopreneurs, indie hackers, 1–3 person agencies, and SMB owners (or the local AI helpers serving them) who already generate drafts with agents or ChatGPT and spend evenings checking them.
- Urgent pain: generation is cheap; review against the actual brand/bar is not. Owners become the bottleneck; managed-service operators cannot scale client approvals.
- Existing workaround: re-read every draft by hand, paste into another model for a second opinion, or ship and hope.

## Why this works

- Market signal: this week’s posts and the Pax8 survey treat review hours as the new scarce resource. OpenWorks and similar programs will increase draft volume without solving the bar problem.
- Agent advantage: ingest examples and drafts, map gaps to concrete rules, produce consistent risk tiers and cited annotations without inventing facts, and surface repeating failure patterns.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has shipped similar work can tighten the checklist language, drop weak annotations, and ship a pack in under 45 minutes of review. Domain judgment is the product; the model is the clerk.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept examples, brand notes, and draft batch. Normalize formats and flag missing detail.

**Agent 2 — Bar extractor.** Pull concrete rules and voice markers from the approved examples. Mark anything inferred.

**Agent 3 — Risk tierer and checklist writer.** Assign risk tiers and draft short checklists. Cite the source examples.

**Agent 4 — Annotator.** Comment on sample drafts against the extracted bar. Every comment cites a source ID or is marked “inferred — confirm.”

**Human operator.** Remove invented facts, drop weak tiers, tighten checklist language, and export Markdown + PDF. Do not approve the client’s drafts or send them on their behalf.

Stack to start: browser/search agents, one LLM, plain Markdown, Stripe Payment Link, mailbox. No product UI until the fifth paid job.

## Risks and mitigations

- Invented rules or false matches: only materials the customer supplies; every claim cites a source ID or is marked “inferred — confirm.” Operator rejects drafts that invent brand facts.
- Over-claiming as legal or compliance review: deliver a research pack and checklist only. Disclaimer on every PDF. High-risk legal claims stay “ask counsel.”
- Thin input: if the customer supplies fewer than three clear examples, return a shorter pack and partial refund rather than invent a bar.
- Overlap with earlier reviewability or verification packs: those are internal agent reliability tools; this pack is specifically client-bar alignment for external AI drafts. Differentiate in sales copy.
- Buyer expects the operator to approve or rewrite the drafts: deliver the pack and stop. Live rewriting or approval is a separate engagement.
