## Concept

Sell a **same-week sticky-portal runbook pack** when a solopreneur, freelancer, clinic, or 1-5 person shop still spends mornings inside Amazon Seller, a payer prior-auth site, a payroll/tax portal, a marketplace helpdesk, or a carrier booking page — and every agent demo dies at the login wall or invents a field.

The customer uploads or points at:

- the 1-3 portals they still click by hand (URLs only; they keep the passwords)
- last 7 days of failed agent traces, screenshots, or "I clicked this myself" notes
- the fields they are allowed to fill vs the fields that require a human or a source document
- optional: one blow-up (agent submitted a rationale not in the chart; selector 404; session expired mid-form)

Agents plus one human operator return within 48 hours:

1. A sticky-portal floor: each portal tagged `agent-safe-this-week` / `human-only-login` / `selector-drift` / `invent-risk`.
2. A runbook card per portal (max 3 in pack one): login ritual the human keeps, field map, documents that must be attached, codes/fields the agent must never invent, pause conditions.
3. A last-7-day receipt: minutes they admitted spending in the portal vs minutes an agent actually completed a submit. Numbers only from their files or marked `draft-unverified`.
4. A 7-line morning card: which portal to open first, which field is blocked without a source file, what stays human, what an agent may pre-fill offline.
5. A first-week script: screenshot the three happy-path pages tonight; pin one "never invent this field" line in the agent harness; do not store their password in the pack.
6. A source appendix. No claim that the agent "owns the portal now."

This is **not** a RPA product, **not** a password manager, and **not** unattended computer-use on a government or payer site. It is a productized desk for *one shop, one week, three sticky portals*, so "the agent will just fill it" becomes a file with a human gate.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US freelancers, indie shops, clinics, and 1-5 person ops teams who already tried a browser agent and still keep a bookmark folder of portals.
- Urgent pain: the agent dies on a changed selector, or worse, submits a field that was not in the source file; the owner spends 40 minutes clicking the same three sites every Monday.
- Existing workaround: do it by hand, paste screenshots into ChatGPT, or buy another automation tool that also breaks next week.

## Why this works

- Market signal: this week's X feed named digital employees, selector drift, invented prior-auth text, leftover small-business portal work, and OOO-dead outreach as the same pattern — agents act until the last hostile website. Existing catalog packs finish one form session, self-heal n8n graphs, and write approval lanes. They do not freeze *this week's* three portals into a cited runbook.

- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has been burned by a bad submit can mark a field `human-only` in five minutes. Judgment about invent-risk is the product; the model is the clerk.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept URLs, screenshots, traces, field lists. Detect one shop and at most three portals. Refuse password dumps and live session cookies.

**Agent 2 — Portal mapper.** Build a page-and-field inventory from screenshots and public docs. Tag invent-risk fields. Never log in as the customer.

**Agent 3 — Drift + failure matcher.** Compare this week's traces to the happy-path map. Mark selector-drift vs login-wall vs missing-source.

**Agent 4 — Pack writer.** Floor, runbook cards, 7-day receipt, morning card, first-week script. Every number cites a file or `draft-unverified`.

**Human operator.** Kill fake field names, strip secrets, export PDF + Markdown. The model does not submit the portal and does not store credentials.

Stack to start: coding + screenshot agents, one LLM, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. Screenshots and URL lists only in week one. No unattended computer-use on payer or government sites.

## Risks and mitigations

- Acting as their RPA vendor: never log into their portal. Draft the runbook. They keep the password.
- Overlap with form-wall finish (2026-09-13): that pack finishes *one live session*. This pack freezes *three recurring portals* for the week.
- Overlap with self-heal diff (2026-09-26): that pack patches n8n/Zapier/Make graphs. This pack covers sites those graphs cannot hold.
- Overlap with approval-lane (2026-09-19): that pack is who may click Send. This pack is which portal field may be filled at all.
- Overlap with overnight ops (2026-09-25): that pack is the shop floor scorecard. This pack is the hostile website leftover.
- Secret-stuffed screenshots: strip credentials, patient names, and tokens before processing. Halt if live cookies appear.
- Computer-use on government or payer sites: week one is runbooks only. Do not let an agent "find another way in" when a portal blocks it.
