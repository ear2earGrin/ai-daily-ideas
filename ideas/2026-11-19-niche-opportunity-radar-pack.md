Existing catalog packs cover overnight ops floors, invoice chasing, and approval lanes. This owns the *morning opportunity radar* for a specific niche.

## Concept

Sell a **same-day niche opportunity radar pack** to solo founders, indie hackers, and small AI/SaaS builders who are drowning in X noise but missing the three signals that actually matter each morning: relevant launches they can participate in, buyer questions they can answer, and competitor moves they should react to.

The customer provides:

- 5–10 niche keywords and competitor account handles
- Preferred sources (X lists, specific HN tags, subreddits, creator deal platforms)
- What "opportunity" means to them (creator deals, partnership gigs, feature gaps, content angles, tool price drops)
- Optional: their current product or content focus

Agents plus one human operator return within 24 hours:

1. A configured source map and query pack (X advanced searches, HN, web).
2. A sample 7-day radar PDF/Markdown with every item cited and scored (relevance 1-10, urgency, suggested action).
3. A morning delivery template (email, Telegram, or Notion) that surfaces only high-signal items.
4. One "first action" card: the single highest-leverage move from the sample week.
5. Setup instructions so they can run it themselves or hand it to their own agents.
6. Source appendix. No invented deals or fake urgency.

This is **not** a hosted always-on agent that posts for them. It is a productized setup + sample so the solo builder has a working radar on day one.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US solo founders, indie hackers, and AI tool builders who already ship something and check X daily but feel they miss deals and signals.
- Urgent pain: scrolling for 45 minutes and still missing the creator launch deals or the exact buyer complaint their product solves.
- Existing workaround: manual X lists, generic AI briefs, or pasting "what’s new in my niche" into ChatGPT every morning.

## Why this works

- Market signal: this week’s X feed is packed with scheduled agents for briefs, job hunts, price watches, and creator deal platforms. People are already trying to build these loops; they want them configured for *their* niche.
- Agent advantage: run parallel searches across X, HN, and deal platforms; score and cite; refuse to invent opportunities.
- Solo-operator advantage: one person who has watched a relevant launch deal go to someone else can tune the scoring and write the first-action card. Taste about what is actually actionable is the product.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept keywords, handles, sources, and opportunity definition. Refuse niches that are too broad ("AI" alone).

**Agent 2 — Source mapper.** Build the X advanced searches, HN queries, and deal-platform checks. Test them and return only live results.

**Agent 3 — Radar writer.** Run the queries, score items, write the 7-day sample with citations and suggested actions.

**Agent 4 — Delivery packager.** Format the morning template and the ready-to-paste scheduled-agent prompt.

**Human operator.** Kill low-signal noise, verify one or two citations, export the pack. The model does not post or apply to anything.

Stack to start: coding + browser agents, one LLM with web/X access, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. No customer logins required.

## Risks and mitigations

- Inventing opportunities: every item must cite a post, listing, or page. Mark unverified as `draft`.
- Overlap with generic news briefs: this is niche + scored + actionable, not a general AI digest.
- Scope creep into running the agent forever: deliver the setup and sample. Retainer is optional.
- Privacy: never store or publish the customer’s full account list.
