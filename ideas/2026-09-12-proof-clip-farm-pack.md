## Concept

Sell a **same-week proof-clip farm pack** when a local shop has work photos and no posting habit.

The customer (trade shop, salon, clinic exterior, cafe, studio, cleaner) uploads:

- 6-12 photos or short phone videos from real jobs (before/after, van, bench, finished wall)
- shop name, city, services they are allowed to name
- a do-not-say list (client faces, addresses, license plates, medical detail, competitor names, invented reviews)
- optional: one competitor Reel they like, brand colors, the one sentence they always say on site

Agents plus one human operator return within three days:

1. A farm card: niche, visual style rules, banned shots, three hooks that are actually in the photos.
2. Seven clip specs: crop, overlay text, caption, first-comment CTA, source photo ids. No invented testimonials.
3. Seven ready-to-post files (static carousel or 8-12s cut) plus a one-week calendar.
4. A character bible page: recurring visual (van / bench / hoodie), voice, what never changes.
5. A source appendix. Every on-screen claim cites a photo, a service list line, or `not-in-record`.

This is **not** a full media company, **not** auto-posting to TikTok, and **not** a fake UGC factory. It is a clerk that turns the camera roll into seven honest posts the owner can publish before they pay an agency.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US local shops with a Google profile and a dead Instagram who already photograph jobs for the client and never post them.
- Urgent pain: a competitor's Reels own the local keyword, the owner has 400 photos in a WhatsApp album, and they will not sit down with CapCut on Sunday.
- Existing workaround: hire a 600/month social kid who invents captions, or post nothing, or paste ChatGPT that claims five-star results the photos do not show.

## Why this works

- Market signal: this week's X thread treats agent media farms as the new default. The catalog already replies to 1-stars and harvests closed jobs. The *outbound proof farm* is still unowned.
- Agent advantage: cluster photos by job, refuse to invent a result that is not in the frame, remix a public format without copying a competitor's face or text.
- Solo-operator advantage: one operator who has posted local work can tell a legal before/after from a privacy leak. Judgment is the product; the model is the clerk.

1. Riskiest assumption: a shop will pay ~149 for seven cited clips instead of asking a nephew to "make them viral."
2. Demand test: post one anonymized sample pack on X and in 3 trade / salon / cafe rooms. DM 25 owners who posted empty grids, "I should post more," or agency-price complaints. Offer the first 5 packs at 99 / 5-day SLA.
3. Success bar: 5 serious replies and 2 paid packs in 14 days. Kill if 25 outbound touches produce zero deposits.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Normalize photos, strip EXIF GPS if present, detect faces and plates. Refuse jobs with only stock images.

**Agent 2 — Format scout.** Pull three public winning formats in that local niche. Extract hook / overlay / CTA structure. Do not copy text or faces.

**Agent 3 — Claim clerk.** For each photo cluster, list what may be said. Mark `not-in-record` for any result, price, or review that is not in the upload.

**Agent 4 — Farm drafter.** Write seven specs, captions, overlay text, and the character bible. Generate the stills or cuts. Every claim cites a source id.

**Human operator.** Kill face/plate leaks, invented five-star lines, and medical claims. Export a zip + a Notion/PDF calendar. The model does not post.

Stack to start: coding + image agents, one LLM, CapCut or ffmpeg for cuts, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. No TikTok login until the fifth paid job. No posting on their behalf in week one.

## Risks and mitigations

- Acting as their social manager: sell a pack. The owner posts. Do not connect TikTok or Instagram in v1.
- Client privacy: blur faces, plates, house numbers. If a face cannot be cropped out, drop the shot.
- Invented social proof: never write "our customers say" unless they uploaded the review. Send review asks to 2026-09-06.
- Overlap with review ambush: if the pain is a live 1-star, send them to 2026-09-11. This pack is outbound proof, not defense.
- Agency-is-cheaper objection after month two: the product is the cited farm plus the bible, not unlimited posting. Lead with the source appendix.
