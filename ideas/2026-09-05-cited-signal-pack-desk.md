## Concept

Sell a **48-hour cited signal pack** for one niche.

The customer (solo founder, studio, or specialist) names:

- the niche and three competitor or creator accounts they respect
- their own last 10 posts or newsletter issues (voice sample)
- what they sell and who they sell it to
- optional: Google Search Console export or a list of pages they want traffic to

Agents plus one human operator return:

1. A signal ledger: 8–12 posts/articles from the last 7 days, each with URL, date, engagement, and a one-line *why it matters*.
2. A cluster map: 3 themes the niche is actually arguing about, not generic "AI is changing everything."
3. Five draft posts in the customer's voice. Every claim has a source id or is marked `operator-opinion`.
4. One 180–220 word email that can go to an existing list the same week.
5. Two "do not post" notes: claims that would be invented, competitor-bashing that would look cheap, or topics already used last week.
6. Optional page-two: three directory or newsletter angles (Cody's pattern) if they want a media asset later.

This is **not** a ghostwriting retainer and **not** an unattended post farm. The model never publishes. The owner copies, edits, and hits send.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/US solo B2B operators (tools, legal-adjacent services, vertical SaaS, specialist agencies) who already have something to sell and freeze when it is time to post.
- Urgent pain: they know distribution is the bottleneck, they watch agent-media threads, and they still open a blank composer.
- Existing workaround: ChatGPT "write 7 tweets about my product," a VA who invents urgency, or silence for three weeks.

## Why this works

- Market signal: this window's X feed treats distribution as the stated failure mode while celebrating agent media companies. Bookmarks cluster on remix-from-outliers, not on more code generation.
- Agent advantage: scan X + a few RSS/changelog sources, refuse unsourced claims, match a short voice card.
- Solo-operator advantage: one maker who already scans X for this repo can productize the same muscle. Judgment is which cluster is real; the model is the clerk.

## Monetization

- Primary model: weekly pack.
- First price test: 79 EUR for one niche / one week; 249 EUR for a 4-week block. 48-hour SLA after intake.
- Upsell: 29 EUR rush (same day); 19 EUR voice-card refresh; 39 EUR "reply pack" (10 draft replies to the week's outlier posts); 390 EUR/month retainer for a studio that wants two niches.
- Fun / public variant: publish one anonymized or own-brand pack each week as proof. That pack is also this GitHub catalog's raw material.

## Validation plan

1. Riskiest assumption: an operator will pay 79 EUR for a cited week of posts instead of prompting a chatbot for free.
2. Demand test: ship one public sample pack (this week's X scan for "solo + AI agents") on X and in two founder Discords. DM 20 people who posted "I need to post more" or bookmarked Cody's media-company thread. First 5 packs at 49 EUR.
3. Success bar: 5 serious replies and 2 paid packs in 14 days. Kill if 25 outbound touches produce zero deposits.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Collect niche, voice samples, destination links, competitor handles.

**Agent 2 — Scanner.** Pull last-7-day X posts + 3–5 web sources. Store URL, date, engagement, raw text.

**Agent 3 — Clusterer.** Group into 3 themes. Drop generic AI hype unless the customer sells AI hype.

**Agent 4 — Drafter.** Five posts + one email. Footnote every factual claim.

**Agent 5 — Voice check.** Diff against the voice card. Flag phrases the customer has never used.

**Human operator.** Kill invented stats, pick the week's posture, refuse to auto-publish.

Stack to start: X search tools already in use, one LLM, a Notion or mailbox inbox, Stripe Payment Link, a simple Markdown pack. No scheduler integration until week four. No multi-account farms in v1.

## Risks and mitigations

- Sounding like a content farm: lead with citations and a "do not post" list. Operator rewrites any fake swagger.
- Platform ToS / multi-account spam: v1 is a file the *customer* posts from their own account. No farm.
- Stale or wrong sources: every line has a URL or is marked opinion. Packs older than 8 days are discounted, not sold as fresh.
- Overlap with trend-report idea (2026-05-18): that SKU is a broader content bundle. This SKU is one week, five posts, one email, all cited.
- Confidential voice samples: isolated processing, delete after 30 days unless they opt in.
