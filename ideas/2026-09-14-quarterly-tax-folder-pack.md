## Concept

Sell a **same-week quarterly estimated-tax folder pack** when a freelancer or tiny studio has a receipt dump and a payment date on the calendar.

The customer uploads:

- payout exports (Stripe, PayPal, Upwork, Wise, bank CSV)
- sent invoices and paid/unpaid flags
- expense folder or photo dump (software, contractors, travel, home office notes)
- last year's return summary or prior quarterly worksheet if they have one
- optional: entity type, jurisdiction, VAT/GST number, CPA email

Agents plus one human operator return within 48 hours:

1. A quarter card: period, cash-in, cash-out, unpaid invoices flagged as *not collected*.
2. An income ledger grouped by source with source-file ids.
3. An expense ledger tagged `likely deductible / needs receipt / personal-looking / ask CPA`.
4. A missing-document list: W-9/1099 equivalents, VAT invoices, foreign payout FX notes.
5. A one-page estimated-payment worksheet: suggested *conversation starter* number range, not a filed return. Every figure cites a row id or `not-in-record`.
6. A send pack: zip structure a CPA will open, plus a 8-line email the freelancer can paste.

This is **not** a tax return, **not** legal or tax advice, and **not** a filing agent. It is a productized clerk that turns a Camera Roll of receipts into a folder a human advisor can price in one sitting.

## Target user

- Primary buyer: EU/UK/US freelancers, indie hackers, and 1-3 person studios who invoice in cash and skip bookkeeping until mid-September or mid-January.
- Urgent pain: estimated payment or accountant deadline is this week; the books are a Downloads folder; ChatGPT invented a number last quarter and they do not want to repeat it.
- Existing workaround: dump the folder on a CPA at 2x the fee, guess a round number, or skip the quarter and pay the penalty later.

## Execution plan (one person + agents)

**Agent 1 — Intake.** Accept zip/CSV/PDF/JPG. Detect platforms and period. Refuse to invent a quarter if dates span two periods without a split rule.

**Agent 2 — Income matcher.** Merge payouts to invoices. Flag paid / pending / missing invoice.

**Agent 3 — Expense classifier.** OCR + vendor guess. Tag deductible-looking vs personal-looking vs needs-receipt. Never claim a code section.

**Agent 4 — Folder drafter.** Build ledgers, missing list, worksheet range labeled *discuss with advisor*, CPA email. Every cell has a source id or `not-in-record`.

**Human operator.** Strip invented tax law, drop medical or other sensitive extras the client did not mean to send, export zip + PDF. The model does not file anything and does not email the tax authority.

Stack to start: coding + browser agents, one LLM, basic OCR, Stripe Payment Link, a mailbox. No connection to IRS/HMRC portals in week one. No auto-pay.

## Risks and mitigations

- Unauthorized tax practice: sell a research folder and a *discussion range*. Do not file, do not compute a penalty as a legal conclusion, name the jurisdiction, put a disclaimer on every PDF.
- Hallucinated deductions: tags are `likely / needs receipt / ask CPA`, never a statute citation unless the operator is admitted/licensed to give that advice in that market.
- Overlap with invoice chase: unpaid invoices stay in a separate tab and are not treated as cash-in. If the pain is collections, send them to 2026-09-07.
- Overlap with inference receipts: model bills are one expense vendor, not a client-facing receipt product.
- Sensitive documents: isolated processing, delete after 30 days unless they opt in to CPA handoff.
- Deadline theater: if intake lands after the statutory date, the pack is still a folder, not a late-file service.
