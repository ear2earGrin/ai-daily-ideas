## Summaries

### [Same-Week Agent Runaway Guard Pack for Solo Operators](ideas/2026-11-19-agent-runaway-guard-pack.md)

A one-person operator plus agents turns a solopreneur’s agent stack and recent logs into a cited runaway-guard pack of hard spend caps, retry limits, kill-switch rules, and paste-ready config or wrapper snippets so an overnight retry loop cannot turn a $400 plan into a multi-thousand-dollar bill.

### [Same-Week Agent Token Efficiency Audit Pack for Solo Operators](ideas/2026-11-18-agent-token-efficiency-audit-pack.md)

A one-person operator plus agents turns a solopreneur’s recent agent usage logs or vendor exports into a cited token-efficiency audit pack of waste patterns, model-routing rules, caching and retry fixes, and paste-ready wrappers so the solo cuts the bill without cutting the useful work.

### [Same-Week Multi-Agent Reviewer Gate Pack for Solo Operators](ideas/2026-11-17-agent-reviewer-gate-pack.md)

A one-person operator plus agents turns a solopreneur’s multi-agent stack (especially customer-facing bots) into a cited reviewer-gate pack of roles, shared-context rules, quality checks, and paste-ready reviewer prompts so nothing ships until a second agent or human confirms it matches buyer intent.

### [Same-Week Agent External Dependency Risk Pack for Solo Operators](ideas/2026-11-16-agent-external-dependency-risk-pack.md)

A one-person operator plus agents turns a solopreneur’s list of external sites, APIs, or platforms their agents automate against into a cited risk pack of known or probable ban/expiry risks, with human fallback steps, so the solo knows which automations have an expiry date they don’t control.

### [Same-Week Agent Daily Digest Pack for Solo Operators](ideas/2026-11-15-agent-daily-digest-pack.md)

A one-person operator plus agents turns a solopreneur’s agent logs, tool outputs, and decision points into a cited daily or weekly digest so the solo sees what the agents did, what needs a human call, and what touched revenue without monitoring a live flood of messages.

### [Same-Week Agent Offline Fallback Runbook Pack for Solo Operators](ideas/2026-11-14-agent-offline-fallback-runbook-pack.md)

A one-person operator plus agents turns a solopreneur’s current agent setup and critical business paths into a cited offline fallback runbook so the solo can still complete refunds, reorders, invoices, and key handoffs by hand when the agent is switched off, out of credits, or down.

### [Same-Week Pre-Call Client Context Brief Pack for Freelancers](ideas/2026-11-13-pre-call-context-brief-pack.md)

A one-person operator plus agents turns a freelancer’s recent emails, portal notes, open invoices, and past call scraps into a cited one-page pre-call brief plus agent-safe vault rules so the solo (or their personal browser agent) walks into the call prepared without live scraping or invented history.

### [Same-Week Agent Silent-Failure Watch Pack for Solo Operators](ideas/2026-11-06-agent-silent-failure-watch-pack.md)

A one-person operator plus agents turns a solopreneur’s scheduled or background agent setup into a cited silent-failure watch pack of heartbeat rules, expected-run windows, miss detectors, escalation channels, and paste-ready prompt/wrapper language so overnight or triggered jobs stop vanishing without a signal.
