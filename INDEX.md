### [Same-Week Agent Offline Fallback Runbook Pack for Solo Operators](ideas/2026-11-14-agent-offline-fallback-runbook-pack.md)

A one-person operator plus agents turns a solopreneur’s current agent setup and critical business paths into a cited offline fallback runbook so the solo can still complete refunds, reorders, invoices, and key handoffs by hand when the agent is switched off, out of credits, or down.

### [Same-Week Pre-Call Client Context Brief Pack for Freelancers](ideas/2026-11-13-pre-call-context-brief-pack.md)

A one-person operator plus agents turns a freelancer’s recent emails, portal notes, open invoices, and past call scraps into a cited one-page pre-call brief plus agent-safe vault rules so the solo (or their personal browser agent) walks into the call prepared without live scraping or invented history.

### [Same-Week Agent Silent-Failure Watch Pack for Solo Operators](ideas/2026-11-06-agent-silent-failure-watch-pack.md)

A one-person operator plus agents turns a solopreneur’s scheduled or background agent setup into a cited silent-failure watch pack of heartbeat rules, expected-run windows, miss detectors, escalation channels, and paste-ready prompt/wrapper language so overnight or triggered jobs stop vanishing without a signal.
